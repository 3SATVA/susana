=escabeche-de-gallina | Pickled Hen in Onion-Vinegar Sauce
- 6 pieces of stewing hen
- 2 large onions, cut in thick slices
- 4 tablespoons vinegar
- 1 cup dry white wine
- 1 tablespoon ground ají
- oregano, salt and pepper
- 2 fresh ají peppers, in strips
- 1/2 cup oil
- olives, hard-boiled eggs and lettuce
> SIMMER: the hen pieces with salt, pepper and water.
> HEAT: the oil in a saucepan with the onions, fresh ají, ground ají, oregano, salt and pepper; cook a few minutes, then add the wine and vinegar.
> ADD: the hen pieces once the sauce is ready; turn out onto a platter and garnish with lettuce leaves, egg and olives.
=escabeche-de-pato | Pickled Duck
- 1 young duck, boiled and cut into pieces
## SAUCE
- 2 large onions
- 4 fresh ají peppers, ground
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1/4 cup vinegar
- 2 fresh ají peppers, in strips
- oil, salt, pepper, cumin, lettuce, hard-boiled eggs, olives
> FRY: the ají and garlic in oil; add the onions cut in large pieces and bring to a boil.
> ADD: the vinegar, season, and boil a few minutes.
> COVER: the duck with this sauce and garnish with hard-boiled eggs, lettuce and olives.
=escabeche-de-pescado | Pickled Fried Fish

- pepper and cumin
- oil for frying

- 2 large onions

=flan-de-alcachofas | Artichoke Custard
- 1/4 cup margarine
- 6 large artichokes, boiled
- 6 eggs
- 1 large can evaporated milk
- 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese
- pepper
- salt
## WHITE SAUCE FOR COVERING
- 2 tablespoons margarine
- 2 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 2 cups milk
- salt and pepper
- 3 tablespoons Parmesan cheese
> SCRAPE: the flesh from the cooked artichoke leaves, carefully clean the hearts and chop finely.
> MIX: the eggs in a bowl; add the milk, salt, pepper, melted margarine, cheese and artichokes.
> POUR: into a greased Pyrex dish and bake at 325ºF (150ºC) in a water bath (baño maría) until set.
> UNMOLD: when almost cool and cover with the following sauce.
> MELT: the margarine in a saucepan, add the flour, milk, salt and pepper; boil one minute and remove.

=jamon-del-pais | Peruvian-Style Boiled Pork "Ham"
- 2 1/4 lb (1 kilo) pork (loin or leg)
- 1 tablespoon crushed garlic
- salt
- achiote (annatto)
- cumin, oregano
- water (to cover the meat)
- 1 onion, sliced
> TIE: the meat to give it a good shape; make small cuts and push in the garlic mixed with salt, achiote, cumin and oregano; let rest 2 hours.
> BRING: water to a boil in a pot, add the meat and lower the heat so it cooks slowly.
> PIERCE: the meat with a fork; if the juice runs clear, it is done.
> REMOVE: let cool slightly and serve with salsa criolla.
> NOTE: this is the essential ingredient of a good butifarra (Peruvian ham sandwich).

=ocopa-a-la-arequipena | Arequipa-Style Ocopa (Potatoes in Ají–Huacatay Sauce)
- 8 yellow ají mirasol peppers
- 3 cloves garlic, charred
- 1 small onion, charred
- 1/2 lb (1/4 kilo) small shrimp (camarones)
- oil
- 7 oz (200 g) shelled walnuts
- 3 vanilla cookies
- 7 oz (200 g) queso fresco
- 1 small can evaporated milk
- a few sprigs of huacatay (Peruvian black mint)
- 1/2 lime
- 3 hard-boiled eggs
- 8 olives
- boiled potatoes
- lettuce leaves
> SOAK: the ají peppers the day before, changing the water several times.
> BLEND: the ají, onion and garlic with enough oil to cover the blender blades.
> FRY: the shrimp heads in a little more oil, blend them too and add the remaining ingredients to make a very smooth cream; season.
> BOIL: the shrimp tails for three minutes and peel them. Serve the sauce over the boiled potatoes, garnished with the hard-boiled eggs, olives and shrimp tails.

- 1/3 loaf sandwich bread (crusts removed)

- 2 bunches Swiss chard, cooked
- 5 bunches spinach, cooked
- 1/2 loaf sandwich bread, crusts removed
- 1 large can evaporated milk
- 3 tablespoons margarine
- 1 small brain (beef or lamb), boiled
- 8 eggs
- 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese

- salt, pepper and oregano

=pastel-de-atun | Tuna Loaf
- 1 can tuna
- 3 tablespoons breadcrumbs
- 2 tablespoons chopped onion
- 2 tablespoons finely chopped parsley
- 4 eggs
- pepper and salt (if needed)
## WHITE SAUCE
- 3 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 1 cup hot milk
- 3 tablespoons margarine
- salt and pepper
> FLAKE: the tuna well in a bowl with a fork.
> ADD: the breadcrumbs, parsley, onion and the eggs one at a time, stirring so the mixture is very smooth.
> MAKE: the white sauce and add it to the mixture, seasoning with salt and pepper.

> SPRINKLE: with breadcrumbs, pour in the mixture, then top again with breadcrumbs and bits of margarine.

> SERVE: with vegetables sautéed in butter.
=pastel-de-alcachofas | Artichoke Pie
## PASTRY
- 1 1/2 cups Nicolini "Harina Preparada" (self-rising flour)
- 1/2 cup margarine
- 4 to 5 tablespoons salted ice water
## FILLING
- 10 to 12 cooked artichokes
- 2 tablespoons margarine
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 1/2 cups milk
- 5 eggs
- 5 tablespoons Parmesan cheese
- salt and pepper
> SIFT: the flour and cut in the margarine with a pastry blender; add the salted water and knead just enough to form a ball.
> DIVIDE: in two halves; roll out the dough and line a 10-inch (25 cm) springform pan.
> MIX: the eggs in a bowl with the milk, Parmesan, the chopped onion (fried first in margarine) and the artichokes (flesh from the leaves and the hearts in pieces); season well and pour over the pastry; cover with the rest of the dough.
> BRUSH: the top with egg yolk diluted in water; bake at 350ºF (175ºC) until golden. Serve cold or hot.
=pastel-de-semola-con-salsa-de-queso | Semolina Bake with Cheese Sauce
- 1 cup Nicolini semolina
- 5 eggs
- 1 cup warm milk
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- salt and pepper
- 1/2 cup margarine
## CHEESE CREAM SAUCE
- 2 tablespoons margarine
- 1 heaping tablespoon Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 1 cup milk
- 2 tablespoons Parmesan cheese
- salt, pepper, nutmeg
> BEAT: the egg whites to stiff peaks; fold in the yolks one at a time, then the semolina, milk, cheese and baking powder.
> GREASE: an 8-inch (20 cm) square Pyrex dish, pour in the mixture and cover the top with the melted margarine.
> BAKE: at 350ºF (175ºC) for 40 minutes.
> COVER: with the following sauce:
> MELT: the margarine in a saucepan; add the flour, milk, salt, pepper and nutmeg and boil 1 minute.
> ADD: the Parmesan and serve at once.
