=adobo-de-chancho | Pork in Vinegar–Ají Marinade
- 2 1/4 lb (1 kilo) pork loin
- 1 cup vinegar
- 1 teaspoon achiote (annatto)
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- salt and pepper
- 6 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1/4 cup dried red ají (ají panca), soaked and ground
- 1/3 cup oil
- 2 1/4 lb (1 kilo) sweet potatoes, boiled
> CUT: the meat into even pieces and put in a container with the vinegar, achiote, cumin, salt and pepper; marinate one hour.
> HEAT: the oil in a pot and fry the garlic, ají and the pork with half of its marinade.
> COVER: the pot and cook over low heat until the meat is done.
> SERVE: with slices of sweet potato.
=adobo-de-pescado | Fish in Vinegar–Ají Marinade
- 1 medium toyo (small shark/dogfish)
- salt and pepper
- 1 level teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon crushed garlic
- 1/4 teaspoon oregano
- 1/2 cup vinegar
- 1/4 cup oil
- 1 tablespoon ground red ají
- 2 onions, sliced
- boiled potatoes
> CUT: the fish, once filleted, into pieces and marinate 1 hour with salt, pepper, cumin, garlic, oregano and vinegar.
> HEAT: the oil and fry the ají; remove from heat and arrange the fish, onions and marinade on top.
> COVER: the pot and cook over low heat a few minutes; serve with boiled, peeled potatoes.
> NOTE: the fish's cooking time depends on the thickness of each piece.
=aguadito-de-choros | Mussel and Cilantro Rice Soup
- 4 dozen mussels
- 1/3 cup oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1/2 tablespoon crushed garlic
- 1/2 tomato, peeled and chopped
- salt, pepper, cumin
- ground ají to taste
- 7 cups mussel broth
- 1 cup rice
- 1/2 lb (1/4 kilo) peas
- 1 ear of corn, kernels cut off
- 1 cup cilantro leaves
- 6 yellow potatoes
> WASH: the mussels well, cover with water and bring to a boil; when it boils, remove them, drain, measure 7 cups of liquid and strain well.
> REMOVE: the mussels from their shells, cleaning them carefully.
> HEAT: the oil and fry the onion, garlic, ají, tomato, salt, cumin and pepper; when the aderezo is cooked, add the peas and corn and boil 5 minutes.
> ADD: the broth, the blended cilantro and the potatoes, then the rice.
> SIMMER: over low heat for 17 minutes; just before serving, add the mussels.
=aguadito-de-pollo | Chicken and Cilantro Rice Soup
- 1 large, tender chicken, cut in pieces
- 1/2 cup oil
- 1 cup very finely chopped onion
- 1/2 cup chopped cilantro
- 2 tablespoons ground cilantro
- 1 teaspoon crushed garlic
- ground ají to taste
- salt, pepper
- 7 cups water
- 1 cup rice
- 1 cup peas
- 1 cup corn kernels
- 6 yellow potatoes
- 1 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" chicken bouillon cube
> HEAT: the oil and fry the onion, garlic, ají, salt and pepper; when the aderezo is cooked, brown the chicken pieces well.
> ADD: the peas and corn and let them sweat a few minutes; add the water, bouillon cube, cilantro, potatoes and rice and boil 17 minutes until the chicken is cooked. Serve at once.
=aguadito-de-pato | Duck and Cilantro Rice Soup
- 3 tablespoons oil
- 6 pieces of duck
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 4 tablespoons ground cilantro
- 3 fresh ají peppers, ground
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 1/2 quarts (1 1/2 liters) water
- 1 glass pisco
- 6 yellow potatoes, in pieces
- 1 cup rice
- salt and pepper
- 1 bell pepper, in strips
- 1 whole ají pepper
- 3/4 cup boiled peas
> HEAT: the oil well, brown the duck pieces and remove.
> FRY: the onion with the cilantro, ají, garlic, salt and pepper; when cooked, add the duck pieces, the pisco and the water and simmer over low heat; then add the bell pepper, potatoes and rice, and when everything is cooked, add the peas. Serve at once.
=ajiaco-de-caiguas | Caigua and Potato Stew with Cheese
- 5 caiguas (stuffing cucumbers), cooked and cut in strips
- 1/3 cup oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- garlic, pepper, salt and cumin
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) potatoes, boiled and crumbled
- 1/2 cup water
- 1 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" beef bouillon cube
- 1/2 cup milk
- 5 oz (150 g) crumbled queso fresco
- 4 tablespoons ground ají mirasol
- chopped cilantro

> HEAT: the oil and fry the onion, garlic, pepper, salt, cumin and ají; when the aderezo is ready, add the water, bouillon cube, milk, cheese and caiguas, bring to a boil, then add the potatoes.

- salt and pepper

=arroz-con-aceitunas | Olive Rice with Cheese Sauce
- 4 tablespoons lard
- 1/2 tablespoon crushed garlic
- 12 oz (350 g) botija olives, blended
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) rice
- ground ají to taste
- 3 1/2 cups water
- salt
## CHEESE SAUCE
- 2 tablespoons margarine
- 2 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 2 cups milk
- salt and pepper
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- hard-boiled eggs
> RICE: heat the lard, brown the garlic, ají and salt; add the water and, when it boils, the rice and olives.
> COVER: the pot and cook 17 minutes.
> MELT: the margarine in a saucepan, stir in the flour, milk, salt and pepper and boil 1 minute. Remove and add the Parmesan.
> MOLD: the rice and cover with the cheese sauce.

=arroz-con-alcachofas | Rice with Artichoke Cream Sauce
- 1/4 cup margarine
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 teaspoon crushed garlic
- 6 artichokes, boiled
- 2 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
- 3/4 cup Parmesan cheese
- 1 cup evaporated milk
- salt, pepper and chopped parsley
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) rice, cooked fluffy
> HEAT: the margarine and fry the onion, garlic, salt and pepper; when the aderezo is cooked, add the chopped artichokes (scrape the flesh from the leaves with a teaspoon and cut the cleaned hearts into small pieces).
> SEASON: and add the milk, cheese and chopped eggs.
> MOLD: the rice, cover with this sauce and garnish with parsley.
=arroz-con-calamares | Black Rice with Squid
- 2 heaping tablespoons oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 1/2 teaspoons crushed garlic
- 1/2 lb (1/4 kilo) shelled peas
- 1 bell pepper, in strips
- 2 dozen squid
- 1 teaspoon lime juice
- salt and pepper
- 1 tablespoon chopped cilantro
- 1 tablespoon ground ají
- 3 cups rice
> CLEAN: the squid.
> FRY: the onion and garlic, add the squid ink, season, and add the peas, ají, bell pepper strips and chopped cilantro; let them sweat a few minutes.
> ADD: the water and lime juice and, when it boils, the rice. Cover and cook over low heat; when it starts to dry, add the squid in rings and continue cooking. Serve at once.
> NOTE: the squid ink is in a small sac inside the body; remove it and strain it into the aderezo.
* The amount of water is not given; about 6 cups for 3 cups rice is typical.
=arroz-con-camarones | Rice with Shrimp Sauce
- 2 1/4 lb (1 kilo) medium freshwater shrimp (camarones)
- 4 tablespoons oil
- 1 teaspoon crushed garlic
- ground ají to taste
- 4 cups rice
## SAUCE
- 4 tablespoons margarine
- 2 tablespoons oil
- 1/2 lb (1/4 kilo) onion, very finely chopped
- 1 large tomato, peeled and chopped
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 1 teaspoon crushed garlic
- salt and pepper
- 4 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
- 1 cup evaporated milk
> WASH: the shrimp and separate the tails; boil the heads 10 minutes in salted water to cover.
> BLEND: then strain.
> FRY: the garlic, ají and salt; add the shrimp broth (4 cups) and a few drops of lime juice, and when it boils add the washed rice; cover and cook 17 minutes.
> HEAT: the oil and margarine and fry the onion, garlic, tomato, tomato paste, salt and pepper; when cooked, blend; return to the pot, add the shrimp tails and boil 3 minutes.
> ADD: the chopped eggs and milk. Serve the rice molded and covered with this sauce.
=arroz-con-chancho | Rice with Pork and Plantain
- 2 tablespoons lard
- 1/2 tablespoon crushed garlic
- ají to taste
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1/2 lb (1/4 kilo) peas
- 2 ears of corn, kernels cut off
- 6 pieces of pork (1 lb / 1/2 kilo)
- salt and pepper
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) rice
- 2 1/2 cups water
- 3 plátanos de isla (ripe plantains), halved
> MELT: the lard and fry the pork pieces well; remove, and make the aderezo with the onion, garlic, salt, pepper and ají.
> RETURN: the meat with the peas and corn, then the water; when it boils add the rice, cover, and when it starts to dry lay the plantain on top. When the rice is fluffy, serve at once.

- 2/3 cup chopped onion
- 1/2 tablespoon crushed garlic
- salt, pepper and cumin
- 2 tablespoons or more ground ají
- 3 tablespoons ground cilantro
- 1/2 tomato, chopped and peeled
- 1 large bell pepper, in strips
- 1 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" chicken bouillon cube
- 3 cups rice

=arroz-con-choros-atamalado | Creamy Rice with Mussels (Atamalado)
- 4 tablespoons lard
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1/2 tablespoon crushed garlic
- salt, pepper, cumin
- ground ají to taste
- 1 cup cilantro leaves (blended)
- 1 ear of corn, kernels cut off
- 3/4 cup boiled peas
- 4 dozen mussels
- green ají pepper, in strips
- 1 bell pepper
- 3 cups rice
- 6 cups mussel broth
> WASH: the mussels and boil them in water to cover. Remove and strain the broth, measure 6 cups, and clean the mussels well.
> HEAT: the lard and fry the garlic, onion, salt, cumin and ají; when cooked, add the bell pepper and ají strips and the corn; cook 5 minutes, add the broth and, when it boils, the rice.

=arroz-con-pato | Rice with Duck, Cilantro and Dark Beer
- 1/3 cup lard
- 3 1/3 lb (1 1/2 kilos) duck, in pieces
- 3/4 cup chopped onions
- 1/2 teaspoon pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon crushed garlic
- 1 cup cilantro leaves, blended
- 1 green ají pepper in strips, ají to taste
- 1 small glass pisco
- 3 cups water
- 1 cup dark beer
- salt and pepper
> HEAT: the lard and fry the duck pieces; remove, and in the same fat brown the onion, garlic, ají, salt, pepper and cumin; add the duck and pisco and boil until the duck is tender; add the water and beer, and when it boils, the rice.
> COVER: the pot and cook 17 minutes over low heat; add the peas and serve at once.
* Rice and peas are used in the steps but not listed; 3 cups rice is typical for this liquid.
=arroz-con-pescado | Rice with Fish
- 1/3 cup margarine
- 2 1/4 lb (1 kilo) white fish
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 tomatoes
- 4 cloves garlic
- 1/2 cup white wine
- 3 1/2 cups fish broth
- 1/3 cup mushrooms
- 3/4 cup peas
- 1 bell pepper, 1 bay leaf
- salt, pepper, paprika
- 2 cups rice
- 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese
> CLEAN: the fish; separate the head and backbone and boil in water to make 3 1/2 cups broth.
> MELT: the margarine and fry the onion, garlic, tomato, salt, pepper and paprika; when cooked, add the peas, bell peppers and blended mushrooms, wine, broth and rice.
> COOK: over low heat and, when it starts to dry, lay the fish on top. Serve sprinkled with Parmesan.
