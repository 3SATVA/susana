=pastel-de-choclo | Sweet Corn and Cheese Bake
- 6 ears of choclo (Peruvian large-kernel corn)
- salt to taste
- 3 tablespoons sugar
- 1/2 cup lard
- 6 eggs
- 1 tablespoon chuño (potato starch)
- 3 teaspoons baking powder
- 1/2 lb (1/4 kilo) queso fresco
- 1/2 cup sugar for sprinkling the top
> GRATE: the corn into a bowl and add the salt, sugar and melted lard, mixing vigorously.
> BEAT: the egg whites to stiff peaks; fold in the yolks, potato starch and baking powder, and add to the corn.
> GREASE: a 10-inch (25 cm) long pan, pour in half the batter, lay slices of cheese on top and cover with the rest.
> SPRINKLE: with sugar and bake at 400ºF (200ºC) for 45 to 50 minutes.
* Choclo is starchier and less sweet than US sweet corn; frozen choclo or fresh sweet corn can be used.
=pastel-de-choclo-con-pollo | Corn Pie with Chicken Filling
- 3/4 cup lard
- 8 tender ears of choclo, kernels cut off
- 1/2 cup milk
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- salt
- 4 eggs
## FILLING
- 1/4 cup margarine
- 1 cup chopped onion
- 1 cup tomatoes
- salt and pepper
- 1 small box raisins
- 2 hard-boiled eggs, sliced
- 8 botija olives
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 small chicken, in pieces
- 1 egg white
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
> BLEND: the corn with the milk; melt the lard and add it to the mixture with the sugar, salt and eggs.
> MAKE: the filling: melt the margarine in a skillet and fry the onion, tomato, salt and pepper; when cooked, add the raisins, eggs, olives, cinnamon and finally the chicken.
> GREASE: a 10-inch (25 cm) square Pyrex dish; pour in half the corn batter, then the filling, then the rest of the batter.
> BRUSH: the top with the egg white beaten with salt. Bake at 350ºF until lightly golden.
=pastel-de-espinacas | Spinach and Egg Pie
## PASTRY
- 1/2 cup margarine
- 2 cups Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 4 tablespoons salted ice water
## FILLING
- 1/4 cup oil
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 3 bunches spinach
- 3 bunches Swiss chard
- 2 tablespoons dried mushrooms, chopped and soaked in warm water
- 1 1/2 cups grated Parmesan cheese
- 1/2 lb (1/4 kilo) queso mantecoso (soft, buttery cheese)
- 6 eggs
- salt
- pepper
- nutmeg
> SIFT: the flour with the baking powder; add the water and salt and work the dough just until it comes together.
> ROLL OUT: with a rolling pin and use two thirds to line an 11-inch (28 cm) removable-bottom pan.
> FRY: the onion, chopped chard and spinach, mushrooms, Parmesan, salt, pepper and nutmeg in the oil; let cool.
> SPOON IN: half the filling, make a few spaced hollows and crack the eggs into them; cover with the rest of the dough and brush the top with yolk diluted in water.
> BAKE: at 400ºF (200ºC) for about 40 minutes.
* The queso mantecoso is listed but not mentioned in the steps; it is presumably layered into the filling.
=pastel-de-fideos-al-graten | Baked Pasta Gratin
- 1/2 lb (1/4 kilo) Nicolini ridged elbows ("codo rayado"), cooked
- 2 cups milk
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 4 tablespoons margarine
- salt
- pepper
- nutmeg
- toasted breadcrumbs
- 4 eggs
> MIX: the eggs in a bowl with the milk, baking powder, Parmesan, two tablespoons margarine, salt, pepper, nutmeg and pasta; combine well.
> GREASE: an 8-inch (20 cm) round Pyrex dish, dust with breadcrumbs, pour in the mixture and top with more breadcrumbs and the remaining margarine in bits.
> BAKE: at 350ºF (175ºC) for about 20 minutes.
> NOTE: for variety, add chopped cooked chicken, chopped ham, mortadella, etc.
=pastel-de-papas | Potato Pie with Pork Filling
- 2 3/4 lb (1 1/4 kilos) yellow potatoes, cooked
- 1/4 cup margarine
- 2 egg yolks
- salt
- pepper
## FILLING
- 2 tablespoons oil
- 1/2 cup chopped onion
- 1/4 cup peeled, chopped tomato
- 1 cup ground pork
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- salt and pepper
- 4 tablespoons chopped parsley
- 1 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" beef bouillon cube
- 1/2 cup hot water
- 1 small box raisins
- 1 hard-boiled egg
- 8 olives
- 1 egg white
> PEEL: the potatoes and put them through a ricer; make a purée with the yolks, margarine, salt and pepper.
> FRY: the onion, tomato, meat, parsley, paprika, bouillon cube, water, salt and pepper in the oil; when cooked, add the raisins.
> GREASE: a 10-inch (25 cm) long Pyrex dish; spread half the purée, top with the filling, sliced egg and olives, and cover with the rest of the purée.
> COVER: with the egg white beaten with salt.
> BAKE: at 400ºF (200ºC) for 10 minutes.
=pastel-de-pescado | Fish Pie
## PASTRY
- 1/2 lb (1/4 kilo) Nicolini "Harina Preparada" (self-rising flour)
- 1/2 cup plus 1 tablespoon margarine
- 1 egg yolk
- 1/4 cup milk
## FILLING
- 2 cups lightly poached fish
- 1/4 cup margarine
- 2 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 1 cup milk
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 tablespoon margarine
- 1 tablespoon onion
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic
- ground ají to taste
- 2 eggs
- salt and pepper
- 1 egg yolk diluted in water
> (PASTRY) COMBINE: the flour with the margarine, yolk and milk; knead just enough to mix and let rest 15 minutes.
> MEANWHILE, MELT: the margarine and fry the onion; add the flour, milk, salt, pepper and Parmesan. In the 1/4 cup margarine, brown the garlic and ají, add the fish and the beaten eggs; mix well, let both mixtures cool, and combine them.
> LINE: a 9-inch (23 cm) springform pan with two thirds of the pastry, add the filling, cover with the rest of the pastry and brush the top with yolk.
> BAKE: at 350ºF (175ºC) for about 45 minutes.
=pastel-de-vainitas | Green Bean Pie
## PASTRY
- 1/2 lb (1/4 kilo) Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 3 teaspoons baking powder
- 3 1/2 oz (100 g) lard
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 2 egg yolks
- ice water (as needed)
## FILLING
- 1/2 cup margarine
- 1/2 teaspoon oregano
- 1 teaspoon crushed garlic
- salt and pepper
- 3 eggs
- 10 1/2 oz (300 g) green beans, finely chopped and cooked
- 1 cup bread soaked in milk
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 egg yolk, diluted
> SIFT: the flour with the baking powder and salt; work in the lard, yolk and enough water to make a soft dough.
> LINE: a 10-inch (25 cm) round pan with half the dough; the filling goes on top.
> MIX: the garlic, oregano, salt, pepper, egg yolks, margarine, green beans, soaked bread and Parmesan in a bowl, and lastly fold in the stiffly beaten whites.
> COMBINE: well, pour in and cover with the rest of the dough.
> BRUSH: the top with yolk. Bake at 450ºF (220ºC) for 20 to 25 minutes.
=pejerreyes-al-horno | Baked Silverside Rolls in Cream Sauce
- 24 pejerreyes (silverside fish)
- 1/2 cup water
- juice of 1/2 lime
- salt and pepper
- 2 tablespoons margarine
- 1 large can evaporated milk
- 2 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- salt and pepper
- 1 tablespoon Parmesan cheese
- chopped parsley
> CLEAN: the fish well, season, roll up (securing with a toothpick) and place in a roasting pan; pour in the water and lime juice and dot with margarine.
> BAKE: at 400ºF (200ºC) for at least 10 minutes.
> REMOVE: drain, and combine this liquid with the milk to make 2 cups.
> HEAT: the margarine, stir in the flour and the milk mixture, salt and pepper; boil 1 minute, then add the cheese.
> ARRANGE: the rolls on a platter, pour the hot sauce over them and sprinkle with chopped parsley.

=picante-de-choclo | Spicy Corn Stew
- 8 ears of choclo, kernels cut off
- 1/4 cup oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 teaspoon crushed garlic
- 1 small tomato, peeled and chopped
- 1 tablespoon ground ají
- 1 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" chicken bouillon cube
- 3 eggs, beaten
- 1 cup evaporated milk
- 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese
- salt and pepper
- 3 hard-boiled eggs
- chopped parsley
> BOIL: the corn in water with a few drops of lime juice and a pinch of sugar; drain.
> HEAT: the oil in a skillet and fry the onion, garlic, ají, tomato and bouillon cube.
> COOK: the aderezo well, then add the corn, milk, eggs, salt and pepper; cook over low heat a few minutes.
> REMOVE: and add the Parmesan. Turn the picante out onto a platter and sprinkle with parsley.
> GARNISH: the edge with potatoes and halved hard-boiled eggs.
* Potatoes are used in the garnish but not listed.

=polenta-con-doble-tuco | Polenta with Tomato Meat Sauce
- 10 cups water
- 5 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" beef bouillon cubes
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) polenta (cornmeal)
- 1/2 cup margarine
- 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 2 or more cans Nicolini "Doble Tuco" (tomato meat sauce)
> PUT: the water and bouillon cubes in a pot; when it boils, add the polenta in a thin stream, stirring constantly, and simmer 20 minutes over low heat.
> REMOVE: from heat and add the margarine and half the Parmesan.
> GREASE: a square mold and pour in the mixture.
> UNMOLD: when firm and cover with the Doble Tuco, heated first in a skillet.
> SPRINKLE: with the remaining Parmesan and serve at once.
=pizza | Pizza (with Variations)
## CRUST
- 1 3/4 cups Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 4 1/2 oz (125 g) butter
- 1/4 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 egg yolk
- warm water as needed
## TOPPING
- 4 tablespoons oil
- 1 cup chopped onion
- 5 tomatoes, peeled, seeded and cut in pieces
- 5 cloves garlic, chopped
- 2 bay leaves, oregano
- 1/2 lb (1/4 kilo) mozzarella cheese
- 4 tablespoons olive oil
## "FOUR SEASONS" PIZZA
- 1/2 cup shrimp, cleaned and cooked
- 1/3 cup mushrooms, halved
- 1/2 cup chopped ham
- 1/3 cup pitted olives
- 1 artichoke heart, cooked
> WORK: the flour and butter together well; add the yolk and enough warm water to make a workable dough.
> ROLL OUT: the dough and line a 12-inch (30 cm) pizza pan.
> HEAT: the oil over low heat, add the garlic and onion; when translucent, add the tomato, bay leaves and a little oregano and fry 10 minutes.
> PLACE: the mozzarella on the raw crust, then the tomato sauce; drizzle with olive oil and bake at 175º (about 350ºF) until the cheese is golden.
> NOTE: the topping above is the basic one for all the pizzas; to vary the flavor, other ingredients are added on top.
> SAUSAGE PIZZA: 1/2 lb (1/4 kilo) sausages cut in small pieces, on top of the basic topping.
> HAM PIZZA: 7 oz (200 g) raw or cooked ham, also cut in small pieces.
> CHEESE PIZZA: 3 1/2 oz (100 g) grated Gruyère.
> ONION PIZZA: 1 large onion, sliced.
> "FOUR SEASONS" PIZZA: make like the others with the ingredients listed.

=salsa-blanca | White Sauce (Béchamel)
- 1 quart (1 liter) milk
- 3 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 1 3/4 oz (50 g) butter
- nutmeg, salt and pepper
> MELT: the butter in a saucepan and add the flour, stirring quickly to prevent lumps.

=salsa-campesina | Pasta with Country-Style Artichoke Sauce
- 6 medium artichokes
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) Nicolini short pasta ("cortos"), cooked
- 1 small can tomato sauce
- 1 onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- a handful of parsley
- 1/2 cup oil
- 1 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" bouillon cube
- 1/2 teaspoon ground ají
- 1 3/4 oz (50 g) grated cheese
- salt to taste
> CLEAN: the raw artichokes down to the bottoms; chop them small and put in cold water with lime juice or salt to keep them white.
> FRY: all the finely chopped vegetables in oil in a saucepan; then add the (well-drained) artichokes and fry together 5 minutes.
> ADD: the tomato sauce, a cup of broth, ají and salt to taste.
> COOK: for 20 minutes, pour over the pasta and serve at once.
=salsa-matrichana | Pasta with Shrimp Cream Sauce ("Matrichana")
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) shrimp (camarones)
- 1 heaping tablespoon butter
- 1 tablespoon oil
- 1/2 teaspoon pepper
- 1 small glass dry sherry
- 1/2 can "crema de leche condensada" (canned cream)
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) Nicolini pasta, cooked
- salt
> BOIL: the shrimp and remove the tails.
> FRY: the shrimp in butter and oil in a small saucepan, then add the sherry, salt and pepper. Remove from the heat and add the cream; just before serving, add 2 tablespoons of the pasta cooking water and sprinkle with grated cheese.
* "Matrichana" is likely a play on Italian "amatriciana", though the sauce is a shrimp cream sauce. "Crema de leche condensada" is ambiguous; canned table cream fits the dish better than sweetened condensed milk.
=salchichas-enrolladas | Hot Dogs Rolled in Thin Omelets
- 4 eggs
- 8 tablespoons milk
- 6 hot-dog sausages
- margarine, mustard
- salt and pepper
> BEAT: the eggs in a bowl and add the milk, salt and pepper.
> PUT: a little margarine in a very small skillet, add a heaping spoonful of the mixture and, when the edges start to brown, carefully flip it with a spatula.
> REMOVE: quickly spread with a little mustard and roll it around a sausage dipped first in boiling water. Serve at once.
