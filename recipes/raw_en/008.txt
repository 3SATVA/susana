=fideos-rellenos | Layered Pasta with Ricotta and Tomato Meat Sauce

- 1 cup evaporated milk
- 2 cups medium white sauce
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) ricotta cheese
- 4 eggs
- 1/2 cup evaporated milk
- 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
> HEAT: the Doble Tuco.
> ADD: 1 cup evaporated milk to the white sauce; break up the ricotta with a fork and mix in the eggs beaten with 1/2 cup milk.

=fideos-con-pollo-al-graten | Chicken and Egg-Noodle Gratin
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) Nicolini egg noodles No. 43, cooked
- 1 chicken, about 3 1/3 lb (1 1/2 kilos)
- 3 egg yolks
- 2 tablespoons dry sherry
- 1 large can evaporated milk
- salt and pepper
- 3 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 4 tablespoons margarine
- 3/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
> POACH: the chicken in lightly salted water to cover; drain and measure 1 1/2 cups broth.
> CHOP: the meat in small pieces (without the skin).
> MELT: the 3 tablespoons margarine, stir in the flour, then the milk and broth; season and boil 1 minute.
> GREASE: a baking dish; add a layer of noodles and cover with the sauce mixed with the chicken, repeating and ending with noodles; sprinkle with Parmesan and dot with the remaining tablespoon of margarine.
> BAKE: at 400ºF (200ºC) for 10 minutes.
* The yolks and sherry are listed but not mentioned in the steps; they are presumably stirred into the sauce off the heat.

=fideos-con-pollo-y-champignones | Baked Egg Bow-Ties with Chicken and Mushrooms
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) Nicolini egg bow-ties ("corbata grande"), cooked
- 3 chicken breasts, cooked and chopped
- 5 oz (150 g) fresh mushrooms
- 1/2 cup margarine
- 2 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- salt and pepper
- 2 eggs
- 1 glass dry sherry
- 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 large can evaporated milk
> SLICE: the mushrooms, dry them well and fry 3 minutes in a tablespoon of margarine.
> MELT: the rest of the margarine; add the flour, milk, salt and pepper and boil 1 minute.
> REMOVE: from heat and add the beaten eggs, sherry, chopped chicken, half the cheese and the mushrooms.
> GREASE: a dish and spread half the pasta, then the sauce, then more pasta; sprinkle with Parmesan.
> BAKE: at 350ºF (175ºC) until golden, taking care it doesn't dry out. Serve at once.

=frijoles-a-la-casilda | Baked Canary Beans "Casilda" with Bacon and Egg
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) canary beans (frijol canario), soaked
- 1 tablespoon margarine
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- parsley, saffron
- 1 small can pimientos
- 1 tablespoon Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 1/2 cup evaporated milk
- 1 teaspoon crushed garlic
- 1/2 teaspoon paprika
- 1/2 cup oil
- 2 hard-boiled eggs
- 4 1/2 oz (125 g) bacon
- 2 tablespoons Parmesan cheese
- ground ají to taste, salt and pepper
> BOIL: the beans; blend half and leave the other half whole.
> FRY: the onion, parsley, saffron and half the pimientos in margarine; add the flour dissolved in the milk and the bean purée, and bring to a boil.
> HEAT: the oil and fry the garlic, paprika, salt, pepper and ají, and lastly the whole beans.
> COMBINE: both mixtures, pour into a Pyrex dish and garnish with the hard-boiled eggs and the cooled bacon.
> BAKE: at 400ºF (200ºC) for 10 minutes, after drizzling with the bacon fat and sprinkling with Parmesan.

=lazana-imperial | Imperial Lasagna with Homemade Pasta
## PASTA DOUGH
- 2 cups Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour) (regular-size cups)
- 3 eggs, salt
## SAUCE
- 1/2 lb (1/4 kilo) ground beef
- 1 3/4 oz (50 g) Italian (white) mushrooms
- 3 1/2 oz (100 g) grated cheese
- 3 1/2 oz (100 g) English-style ham
- 1 white onion
- 1 carrot, grated
- 2 tablespoons oil
- 5 oz (150 g) butter
- 1 can evaporated milk
- 1 handful parsley
- 1/2 cup water
- celery, salt and pepper
> PLACE: the flour in a ring on the board; add the eggs, salt and enough water to make a soft dough.
> ROLL OUT: as thin as possible and cut into rectangles of about 8 inches (20 cm).
> BOIL: in plenty of salted water and dip in cold water so the sheets can be handled.
> LAYER: in a buttered baking dish with a little sauce in the bottom: a layer of pasta, then sauce, strips of ham and Parmesan, repeating until everything is used.
> DRIZZLE: with melted butter and grated cheese and bake until golden, 15 or 20 minutes.
> (SAUCE) FRY: the finely chopped vegetables in half the butter; add the meat and the mushrooms (soaked in hot water and finely chopped), fry a few minutes, then add the milk, water, salt and pepper. The sauce cooks for 20 minutes.

- 2 tablespoons dried mushrooms, chopped and soaked
- 1 bay leaf
