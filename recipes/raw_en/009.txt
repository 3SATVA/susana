=noquis-de-papa | Potato Gnocchi
- 2 1/4 lb (1 kilo) white potatoes, boiled
- 9 oz (250 g) Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- salt and pepper
- 3/4 cup butter
- 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
> PEEL: the potatoes while still hot, put them through a ricer and mix in the flour, salt and pepper.
> KNEAD: just enough to form a ball; roll into a fairly thick rope, cut into 3/4-inch (2 cm) pieces and roll them over a fork.
> HEAT: plenty of salted water and drop in the gnocchi one by one; when they float to the top, they're done.
> LIFT OUT: with a slotted spoon, drizzle with butter and sprinkle with grated Parmesan.
=noquis-de-semola | Baked Semolina Gnocchi
- 1 quart (1 liter) milk
- 7 oz (200 g) Nicolini semolina
- 1/4 cup margarine
- 2 egg yolks
- 2 tablespoons Parmesan cheese
- salt, pepper, nutmeg
> PUT: the milk and margarine in a saucepan; when it boils, sprinkle in the semolina like rain, stirring constantly with a wooden spoon.
> SIMMER: over low heat for 20 minutes; remove and add the cheese, yolks, salt, pepper and nutmeg.
> POUR: the mixture onto a greased sheet pan and smooth with a rolling pin to 3/4 inch (2 cm) thick.
> CUT: once cool, into 2-inch (5 cm) rounds.
> ARRANGE: overlapping in a greased dish, sprinkle with Parmesan and brown in the oven 10 minutes (printed as 400ºF-175ºC).
> SERVE: with heated Nicolini "Doble Tuco" (tomato meat sauce) or a creamy white sauce.
? oven temperature printed as "400ºF-175ºC" (400ºF is about 200ºC)
=olluquitos-con-charqui | Olluco Tubers with Dried Beef
- 1 2/3 lb (3/4 kilo) ollucos (small Andean tubers)
- 7 oz (200 g) charqui (dried beef), toasted and shredded
- 5 tablespoons ground red ají (ají panca)
- 1 onion, chopped
- 3/4 cup oil
- 1/2 tablespoon crushed garlic
- salt, pepper and cumin
> SOAK: the charqui (to soften it) and rinse well.
> FRY: the onion, garlic and ají in oil; when cooked, add the charqui, then the ollucos cut in thin lengthwise strips; season, cover the pot and cook over low heat (without water).
> SPRINKLE: with chopped parsley once cooked.
=pescado-a-la-chorrillana | Fish Chorrillos-Style with Onion and Tomato
- 2 1/4 lb (1 kilo) fish fillets
- salt and pepper
- Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 1/2 cup oil
- 2 large onions, cut in thick strips
- 3 tomatoes, peeled and cut in thick wedges
- 1 green ají pepper, cut in strips
- 1 teaspoon crushed garlic
- salt and pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon oregano
- ground ají to taste
- lime juice
> SEASON: the fish pieces, flour them and fry in hot oil.
> HEAT: the 1/2 cup oil and cook the onion until limp; add the garlic, tomato, oregano, salt, pepper and the ground and sliced ají; lay the fish on top and leave about 5 minutes over low heat.
> SPRINKLE: with lime juice. Serve with boiled potatoes, rice, etc.
=pescado-sudado | Steamed Fish in Onion-Tomato Broth
- 6 fish fillets, about 5 oz (150 g) each
- Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 1/4 cup oil
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 1/2 tablespoon crushed garlic
- 1 tablespoon chopped parsley
- 1 tablespoon chopped cilantro
- 1/4 cup dry white wine
- 1 large tomato, sliced
- salt, pepper, lime juice
> SEASON: the fillets with salt and pepper, dredge in flour and fry quickly in oil.
> HEAT: the oil and cook the onion (without browning); add the garlic, remove from heat and lay in the fillets; sprinkle with parsley and cilantro, place the tomatoes on top of the fillets, and pour in the wine and lime juice.
> COVER: the pot and simmer gently for a few minutes.
! NOTE: the fish cooking time depends on the thickness and quality of the fillet.

=pimientos-rellenos | Stuffed Bell Peppers with Pork
- 6 bell peppers
- 1/8 cup oil
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1 cup ground pork
- 1 hard-boiled egg
- 1 cup water
- 1 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" beef bouillon cube
- 1 teaspoon Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 2 egg yolks
- 2 tablespoons margarine
- salt
- pepper
> CLEAN: the peppers, cut off the tops and hollow them out with a teaspoon.
> HEAT: the oil and brown the onion, paprika and meat; season and add the egg; stuff the peppers with this filling.
> ARRANGE: in a pot and pour in the water with the bouillon cube plus 1 tablespoon margarine; cook over low heat.
> REMOVE: the peppers carefully; to the juices left in the pot add the rest of the margarine and the flour dissolved in a little water, boil 1 minute, then off the heat stir in the beaten yolks.

- 1 sprig celery
- 3 cups boiling water
- 1 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" chicken bouillon cube

=quinua-atamalada | Creamy Quinoa with Dried Shrimp and Cheese

- 1/2 cup oil
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 1 heaping tablespoon special ground red ají (ají panca)
- 1/2 tablespoon crushed garlic

- salt, pepper, oregano, chopped parsley

- 1/2 cup crumbled queso fresco

=salsa-de-hongos | Fresh Spinach Noodles with Mushroom Sauce
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) Nicolini pasta
- 2 cups Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 2 eggs
- 3 bunches spinach
- a little milk
- a pinch of nutmeg
## SAUCE
- 1 package dried mushrooms (1/2 oz / 15 g)
- 1 small onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- a small handful of parsley
- a scant 1/2 glass oil
- 1 tablespoon butter
- 1 small can tomato sauce
- 1/2 can peas
- grated cheese to taste
> KNEAD: the flour, eggs, milk, salt, nutmeg and the boiled, finely chopped spinach on a board until you have a smooth, even dough.
> ROLL OUT: the dough to a medium sheet, dust generously with flour and roll it up; cut to the width you like and let dry a little.
> SAUCE — SOAK: the mushrooms in warm water for about 1/2 hour.
> FRY: all the finely chopped vegetables in butter and oil; add the chopped mushrooms, fry 5 minutes more, and lastly the tomato sauce.
> COOK: about half an hour.
! the recipe is really fresh spinach noodles with mushroom sauce
* The "1/2 kilo of Nicolini pasta" seems to be an alternative to the homemade noodles.

=sesos-al-horno | Baked Brains under Mashed Potatoes
- 2 tablespoons margarine
- 2 brains
- 1 tablespoon Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 2 tablespoons chopped parsley
- 1 cup milk
- 1 egg
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) yellow potatoes, boiled
- 1 small can evaporated milk
- 1/3 cup margarine
- 1 egg, salt and pepper
> RINSE: the brains well and simmer in water to cover with a small splash of vinegar and salt over low heat for 15 minutes; remove the membrane and chop.
> HEAT: the margarine, add the flour, milk, salt and pepper and boil 1 minute; remove and add the beaten egg, parsley and brains.
> BOIL: the potatoes, peel, put through a ricer and add the melted margarine, salt, pepper, yolk and stiffly beaten white.

=timbal-de-fideos-con-camarones | Baked Egg-Noodle Timbale with Shrimp and Nantua Sauce
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) Nicolini egg noodles (cooked)
- 3 cups medium white sauce
- 6 eggs
- 2 1/4 lb (1 kilo) small freshwater shrimp
- 1 cup fresh mushrooms fried in 1 tablespoon butter
- 1/4 cup margarine
## NANTUA SAUCE
- 2 cups shrimp broth
- salt
- pepper
- 2 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 1 tablespoon margarine
> MIX: in a bowl the eggs with the white sauce, the mushrooms fried 4 minutes in margarine, and the peeled raw shrimp tails.
> ADD: the noodles and combine well.
> POUR: into a greased Pyrex dish and dot with margarine.

> BOIL: 1 minute, season and add the margarine.
=tortelloni-de-vispera | "Eve-of-the-Feast" Spinach-Ricotta Tortelloni
## DOUGH
- 2 1/2 cups Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 3 eggs
- a pinch of salt
## FILLING
- 1/2 lb (1/4 kilo) ricotta
- 1 3/4 oz (50 g) grated cheese
- 2 bunches spinach
- a small handful of parsley
- a pinch of nutmeg
- salt and pepper
## SAUCE
- 1/2 cup fresh tomato sauce
- 3 1/2 oz (100 g) butter
- grated cheese
> MAKE: a dough with the flour and eggs, adding a few tablespoons of cold water if needed so it's soft.
> ROLL OUT: the dough very thin; with a glass cut 1 1/2-inch (4 cm) rounds, place a portion of filling in the center of each, moisten the edge with egg white, fold closed and press with your fingers to seal.
> BOIL: in plenty of salted water; at serving time, dress with the tomato sauce and butter.
* "De víspera" means "of the eve" — traditionally made the day before a feast; title kept as an approximation.
