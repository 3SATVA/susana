=semola-con-doble-tuco | Semolina Bake with Tomato Meat Sauce
- 1 3/4 cups milk
- 1 cup Nicolini semolina
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 2 tablespoons margarine
- 1 egg yolk diluted in water (1/2 tablespoon)
- 1 can Nicolini "Doble Tuco" (tomato meat sauce)
> BOIL: the milk in a saucepan; add the salt, then the semolina in a thin stream, stirring constantly, then the margarine. Cook over low heat for 20 minutes.
> REMOVE: and spread half into a greased 8-inch (20 cm) square mold; cover with the Doble Tuco and add the rest.
> BRUSH: the top with the diluted yolk.
> BAKE: at 350ºF (175ºC) for 15 minutes. Serve at once.
=semola-napolitana | Neapolitan-Style Semolina Squares
- 4 cups milk
- 1 tablespoon margarine
- 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- salt, pepper, nutmeg
- 3 egg yolks
- 1 or more cans Nicolini "Doble Tuco" (tomato meat sauce) or cheese sauce
- 1/2 lb (1/4 kilo) Nicolini semolina
> BOIL: the milk in a pot with salt, pepper and nutmeg.
> ADD: the semolina in a thin stream, stirring constantly until thick; boil 20 minutes.
> ADD: the margarine, yolks and grated cheese; spread on a greased sheet pan, flatten with a rolling pin, let cool and cut squares of the size you like.
> ARRANGE: on a baking sheet, sprinkle with a little Parmesan and bake at 400ºF (200ºC) for a few minutes; serve at once with the heated Doble Tuco.
=souffle-de-coliflor | Cauliflower Soufflé
- 3 tablespoons margarine
- 4 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 1 medium cauliflower
- 1 cup milk
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- salt and pepper
- 4 eggs
> BOIL: the cauliflower in water with milk and a piece of bread; when cooked, drain and chop finely.
> MELT: the margarine in a pot and add the flour, then the milk little by little, stirring constantly; boil 1 minute and season.
> ADD: off the heat the yolks, cheese, cooked cauliflower and finally the stiffly beaten whites.
> GREASE: a mold, pour in the mixture and bake at 350ºF (175ºC) for 40 minutes. Serve at once.
=souffle-de-fideos | Pasta Soufflé
- 3 1/2 oz (100 g) Nicolini spiral pasta ("helicoidal"), cooked
- 2 tablespoons margarine
- 4 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 2 cups hot milk
- salt, pepper, nutmeg
- 3 tablespoons Parmesan cheese
- 5 eggs
> MELT: the margarine in a saucepan, add the flour, then the milk, salt, pepper and nutmeg; boil 1 minute, remove and add the yolks, cheese, pasta and finally the stiffly beaten whites. Fold gently.
> GREASE: an 8-inch (20 cm) round Pyrex dish and pour in the mixture.
> BAKE: at 350ºF (175ºC) until golden.

- 6 bunches spinach, cooked
- 1 can pâté
- 1 large can undiluted evaporated milk
- salt and pepper
- 3 tablespoons margarine
- 4 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 2 cups milk
- 4 tablespoons Parmesan cheese
> BLEND: the spinach with the evaporated milk and pâté, season, and mix with the pasta.

=tamalitos-de-garbanzos | Chickpea Tamales with Pork
## DOUGH
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) chickpeas, soaked overnight
- 1/2 lb (1/4 kilo) lard
- 2 tablespoons ground cilantro
- ground ají to taste
- salt and pepper
- 2 cups of the broth the pork was cooked in
## FILLING
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) pork
- 4 tablespoons lard
- 1 onion, cut in strips
- 4 green ají peppers, in strips
- 3 hard-boiled eggs
- 8 olives
- corn husks (dipped in boiling water to soften)
> GRIND: the chickpeas to a fine paste.
> COOK: the pork in salted water; when tender, cut in small pieces.
> MELT: the lard in a skillet, fry the onions and ají, season and add the pork, olives and eggs.
> HEAT: the lard in a saucepan; add the chickpea paste, broth and cilantro, and season.
> COOK: over low heat until the mixture is thick like tamal dough and glossy.
> ON CORN HUSKS: put a heaping spoonful of the mixture, the filling in the center, then more mixture.
> TIE: carefully and set the tamales in a pot with very little water; boil for 1/2 hour.
=tamalitos-verdes | Green Corn and Cilantro Tamales
- 8 ears of choclo
- 2/3 cup lard
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 1 teaspoon crushed garlic
- ground ají to taste
- 1/3 cup ground cilantro
- salt and pepper
## FILLING
- 2 hard-boiled eggs
- 2 green ají peppers, in strips
- 1/2 lb (1/4 kilo) pork
- olives
- corn husks (dipped in boiled water)
> BLEND: the corn. Melt the lard in a skillet.
> FRY: the garlic, onion and ají and season; when cooked, set a little aside for the filling.
> TRANSFER: the rest to a saucepan, add the blended corn and cilantro, stirring constantly until thick like tamal dough and glossy.
> BOIL: the pork in salted water; when cooked, cut in small pieces and combine with the reserved aderezo, ají, eggs and olives.
> PUT: a spoonful of the mixture on a husk, then filling and more dough; tie.
> STEAM: (in a pot with three fingers of water, lay the corn cobs in the bottom with the tamales on top), cover and cook 20 minutes.
=timbal-de-espinacas | Spinach Timbale
- 6 bunches spinach
- 5 eggs
- 1 cup Parmesan cheese
- 1 cup milk, for soaking:
- 10 slices sandwich bread, crusts removed
- salt, pepper, nutmeg
## WHITE SAUCE FOR COVERING
- 2 tablespoons margarine
- 2 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 2 cups milk
- 3 tablespoons Parmesan cheese
- salt
- pepper
> WASH: the spinach leaves, cook in very little water, drain, rinse in cold water and blend.
> MIX: the eggs in a bowl; add the spinach, Parmesan and the bread blended with the milk; season with salt, pepper and nutmeg.
> GREASE: a long Pyrex dish, add the mixture and bake at 350ºF (175ºC) until set.
> UNMOLD: once warm and cover with the following sauce:
> MELT: the margarine in a saucepan; add the flour, milk, salt and pepper and boil 1 minute.
> REMOVE: and stir in the Parmesan.

- salt and pepper

=cazuela-de-cordero | Lamb and Vegetable Soup
- 2 1/4 lb (1 kilo) lamb
- 8 cups water
- 1 cup chopped green beans
- 6 rounds of corn on the cob
- 1/2 cup chopped carrots
- 1/2 celery heart, chopped
- 1 cup peas
- 1 cup chopped cabbage
- 1 green ají pepper, toasted
- 6 potatoes, peeled
- 1/4 cup oil
- 2 tablespoons rice
- 6 tablespoons very finely chopped onion
- 1/2 teaspoon crushed garlic
- salt and pepper
- cilantro and oregano
> BOIL: the lamb in pieces with the water in a pot; when partly cooked, add the listed ingredients, including the rice.
> HEAT: the oil in a skillet and brown the garlic, onion, oregano and cilantro to make an aderezo.
> SEASON: once well cooked, add it to the soup. Boil 5 minutes more.
> SERVE: hot.

=caldo-de-pescado-con-fideos | Fish Broth with Pasta
- 2 1/4 lb (1 kilo) fish heads, tails and backbones
- 1 large onion
- 1 leek
- 2 stalks celery
- salt and pepper
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 large sprig parsley
- 12 cups water
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 2 tablespoons oil
- 1/2 tomato, peeled and chopped
- ground ají to taste
- oregano
- 1 cup Nicolini small fish-shaped soup pasta ("pescadito")
> BOIL: the fish pieces in a pot with the water, three quarters of the onion in slices, leek, bay leaf, parsley, celery, salt, pepper and wine; skim when it boils and simmer over low heat until concentrated; strain.
> FRY: the rest of the onion, very finely chopped, in the oil; add the tomato, ají and oregano; when cooked, add to the broth.
> ADD: the pasta when the broth comes to a boil.
> COOK: until done.
> SERVE: at once.
