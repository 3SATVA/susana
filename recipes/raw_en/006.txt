=arroz-tapado | Molded Rice with Tomato Meat Sauce
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) rice
- 1 heaping tablespoon lard
- 1 teaspoon crushed garlic
- salt and a few drops of lime juice
- 1 can Nicolini "Doble Tuco" (tomato meat sauce)
- 2 tablespoons raisins
- 6 botija olives, chopped
- 2 hard-boiled eggs, sliced
- 1 tablespoon chopped parsley
> WASH: the rice well and measure it.
> HEAT: the lard and fry the garlic; add the salt, water (the same amount as the rice) and lime juice; when it boils, add the rice, cover and cook over low heat 17 minutes.
> PUT: the Doble Tuco, raisins, olives and chopped parsley in a skillet.
> MOLD: the rice: a layer of rice, a spoonful of the meat mixture, more rice; garnish with a slice of hard-boiled egg.
=arroz-tapado-de-atun | Molded Rice with Tuna Filling
- 2 1/2 cups rice
- 2 1/2 cups water
- 1/2 teaspoon crushed garlic
- salt to taste
- lard
## FILLING
- 1 can tuna
- 1/2 cup chopped onion
- 1/4 cup chopped, peeled tomato
- 1/2 cup oil
- 2 hard-boiled eggs
- 1/3 cup chopped olives
- pepper, cumin, salt to taste
- 1 teaspoon chopped parsley
- a few sprigs parsley
> COOK: the rice until fluffy with lard, garlic and salt to taste.
> FRY: the onion, pepper, cumin and salt in a skillet; add the tomato; once the aderezo is ready, add the flaked tuna, olives and parsley; remove and add 1 chopped hard-boiled egg.
> SPOON: part of the rice into a large Pyrex dish or several individual molds, then the tuna filling, then more rice; unmold and garnish with slices of the other egg and parsley sprigs. Serve hot.
=asado-de-papas | Potato and Rice Soup with Ají Mirasol
- 1/3 cup oil
- 1/2 cup very finely chopped onion
- 1/2 teaspoon crushed garlic
- 1/2 teaspoon paprika
- 1/2 cup peeled, chopped tomato
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- water (8 cups)
- salt and pepper
- 2 tablespoons ground ají mirasol
- 4 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" beef bouillon cubes
- 1 sprig mint (hierbabuena)
- 3 white potatoes, peeled and quartered
- 3/4 cup rice
> HEAT: the oil and fry the onion, garlic, tomato, tomato paste, paprika, salt and pepper.
> ADD: the ají, water, bouillon cubes and mint.
> ADD: the potatoes and rice when the water boils; cook over low heat.
> SERVE: at once.
* Despite the name ("potato roast"), this is a brothy dish with 8 cups water.
=caiguas-rellenas | Stuffed Caiguas
- 2 tablespoons oil
- 6 caiguas (stuffing cucumbers)
- 1 onion
- 10 1/2 oz (300 g) ground pork
- 1 tomato
- 1 teaspoon crushed garlic
- 1 egg
- ground ají to taste
- 2 tablespoons raisins
- salt
- pepper
- 1 tablespoon parsley
- 2 slices bread soaked in milk and blended
- 1/2 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" beef bouillon cube, dissolved in 3/4 cup boiling water
> CUT: a lid off the caiguas and clean them out.
> BROWN: the onion in oil with garlic, tomato, ají, salt and pepper; when cooked, add the ground meat, bread, egg, raisins and parsley, and fill the caiguas with this stuffing.
> ARRANGE: in a pot containing the rest of the stuffing, add the broth and simmer until tender.
=cabrito-al-horno | Roast Kid Goat
- 1/2 cup oil
- salt and pepper
- 1 tablespoon crushed garlic
- 1 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" beef bouillon cube
- 1 kid goat, 11 to 13 lb (5 to 6 kilos)
> MIX: the oil in a bowl with the salt, pepper, garlic and bouillon cube (crush it with a fork).
> RUB: the goat well with this mixture, inside and out.
> PLACE: in a roasting pan and roast at 350ºF (175ºC) for about 1 1/2 hours. Serve with browned potatoes, yuca, beans, etc.
=capeletti | Ham Cappelletti with Walnut Sauce
## DOUGH FOR 6
- 2 cups Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 1 egg
- 1 egg yolk, salt
## FILLING
- 9 oz (250 g) ham
- 2 whole eggs
- 2 tablespoons grated cheese
- salt, pepper, nutmeg
## CREAM SAUCE
- 3 1/2 oz (100 g) butter
- 2 tablespoons oil
- 3 1/2 oz (100 g) grated cheese
- 1/2 lb (1/4 kilo) walnuts (1 cup shelled)
- 1 clove garlic
- a little parsley
- 2 slices bread soaked in milk
- salt and pepper
> DOUGH: PLACE: the flour in a ring on the board.
> ADD: 1 egg and the yolk, knead gently, and roll the dough out as thin as possible.
> CUT: squares about 1 1/2 inches (4 cm) wide and put a little filling in the center of each (ham ground very fine with the 2 eggs and grated cheese).
> FOLD: into triangles and press gently; bring the two ends together around your finger to make ring-shaped cappelletti.
> BOIL: in plenty of salted water, drain well and dress with the walnut sauce.
> SAUCE: CLEAN: the walnuts with boiling water; mash the garlic and gradually add the walnuts, cheese, soaked bread, parsley, oil and butter; season with salt and pepper and mix well to a thick sauce.
=carapulcra | Dried Potato Stew with Pork and Chicken
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) papa seca (dried potato), toasted
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) pork
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) chicken or stewing hen
- 1/2 cup lard
- 1 cup chopped onion
- 1 tablespoon crushed garlic
- 1/4 cup ají especial (red ají panca paste)
- cumin, salt and pepper
- 3 1/2 oz (100 g) peanuts, toasted and ground
- 1/2 cup "rosquitas de manteca" (lard ring cookies), crushed
- 2 cloves, ground
- 1/2 cup sweet wine
- 1 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" beef bouillon cube dissolved in 2 cups water
> SOAK: the papa seca for 1/2 hour in cold water.
> HEAT: the lard and fry the pieces of meat; in the same fat brown the onion, garlic, cumin, salt, pepper and ají; when cooked, return the meats with the broth, and when it boils add the papa seca, 1/2 cup sweet wine, cloves and peanuts.
> SIMMER: over low heat, stirring constantly so it doesn't stick; when the fat rises to the surface and the papa seca is soft, remove from heat.
> REST: 1/4 hour, then serve piping hot.
* "Rosquitas de manteca" are small lard cookies (see the dessert section); crushed crackers or plain cookies work as a thickener.
=cau-cau-a-la-criolla | Creole Tripe and Potato Stew with Mint
- 2/3 cup oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1/2 tablespoon crushed garlic
- a pinch of cumin
- 1 teaspoon palillo (Peruvian turmeric)
- 2 tablespoons ground ají
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) tripe
- 2 1/4 lb (1 kilo) white potatoes, cooked and chopped
- salt
- 1 sprig mint (hierbabuena), chopped
- lime juice
> SIMMER: the tripe in water to cover with 2 tablespoons milk and a sprig of mint until tender; cut in small squares.
> HEAT: the oil and fry the onion, garlic, cumin, palillo and ají; when cooked, add the tripe and potatoes and bring to a boil.
> ADD: the lime juice and mint just before serving.
> SERVE: with fluffy white rice.
=cau-cau-de-choros | Mussel and Potato Stew with Mint
- 1/2 cup oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1/2 tablespoon crushed garlic
- fresh ají to taste
- 5 dozen mussels
- salt and pepper
- 1 2/3 lb (3/4 kilo) potatoes, boiled, peeled and diced
- 1 sprig mint (hierbabuena), chopped
- lime juice
> WASH: the mussels well and put them in a pot with water to cover; when they boil and open, remove and clean them well.
> HEAT: the oil and fry the onion, garlic and ají; when the aderezo is cooked, add the mussels and potatoes and bring to a boil.
> REMOVE: from heat and add the lime juice and mint. Serve with fluffy white rice.

=col-rellena | Stuffed Cabbage Rolls in Tomato Sauce
- 1 medium cabbage
- 10 1/2 oz (300 g) ground pork
- 3 tablespoons chopped parsley
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 2 slices sandwich bread soaked in milk
- 1 egg, salt and pepper
## SAUCE
- 1/4 cup oil
- 2 tablespoons chopped onion
- 1/2 lb (1/4 kilo) tomatoes
- 1/4 teaspoon sugar
- salt and pepper
> CHOOSE: 12 even cabbage leaves (discarding the outer ones), blanch them, and fill.
> MIX: all the filling ingredients; take each leaf and put a little filling in the center.
> CLOSE: into a small packet secured with a toothpick.
> HEAT: the oil and fry the onion and the other sauce ingredients; when cooked, lay the rolls on top, cover and simmer a few minutes.
=coliflor-saltada | Sautéed Cauliflower with Egg

- 3 tablespoons margarine
- salt and pepper
- 1/2 cup very finely chopped onion
- 1/2 teaspoon crushed garlic
- ground ají to taste
- 4 eggs
- 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese
- chopped parsley
> BOIL: the cauliflower florets in water with a piece of bread; when cooked, drain.

=fideos-con-atun-al-graten | Tuna Pasta Gratin with Ají
- 1/2 lb (1/4 kilo) Nicolini shells ("sorpresa"), cooked
- 5 slices white bread, crusts removed
- 1 cup evaporated milk
- 1/2 cup water
- 1/4 cup margarine
- 1 onion, chopped
- 6 fresh ají peppers, ground
- salt and pepper
- 3/4 cup Parmesan cheese
- 1 can tuna
> SOAK: the bread in the milk and water for 10 minutes and blend.
> MELT: the margarine and fry the onion well; add the ground ají, salt and pepper; when cooked, add the blended bread and cheese and mix well.

> BAKE: at 400ºF (200ºC) for 10 minutes. Serve at once.
=fideos-al-queso | Four-Cheese Baked Noodles
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) Nicolini egg noodles No. 41, cooked
- 3 1/2 oz (100 g) queso mantecoso (soft, buttery cheese)
- 5 oz (150 g) queso fresco
- 3 1/2 oz (100 g) Parmesan cheese
- 3 1/2 oz (100 g) Emmental cheese
- 3 1/2 oz (100 g) margarine
- 2 eggs
- toasted breadcrumbs
- salt
> GREASE: a baking dish and add a quarter of the noodles; top with slices of queso fresco, cover with another equal amount of noodles, and add thin slices of Emmental.
> REPEAT: scattering bits of queso mantecoso, then the last layer of noodles; sprinkle with Parmesan and bits of margarine.
> BEAT: the whites to stiff peaks and fold in the yolks; pour over the noodles and sprinkle with breadcrumbs. Bake at 400ºF (200ºC) until browned. Serve at once.
=fideos-con-camarones-y-huevo-duro | Pasta with Shrimp Cream Sauce and Hard-Boiled Egg
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) Nicolini thick "entrefino" coils, cooked
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) freshwater shrimp (camarones)
- 2 cups of the water the shrimp heads cook in
- 3 tablespoons margarine
- 3 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 2 tablespoons chopped onion
- 1 cup evaporated milk
- 1 cup Parmesan cheese
- 3 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
- salt and pepper
- 1 tablespoon margarine
- 1/2 tablespoon oil
> SEPARATE: the shrimp tails from the heads, and boil the heads 10 minutes in lightly salted water to cover.
> BLEND: and strain; measure 2 cups of liquid.
> HEAT: the margarine and fry the onion; add the flour, salt and pepper, the shrimp liquid and the milk, and boil 1 minute.
> FRY: the peeled shrimp tails 3 minutes in the margarine and oil and add them to the sauce.
> TOSS: the sauce with the pasta, half the Parmesan and the hard-boiled eggs.
> TURN OUT: onto a platter and sprinkle with the remaining Parmesan. Serve at once.
