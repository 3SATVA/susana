=pionono | Rolled Sponge Cake with Manjarblanco
- 6 eggs
- 2/3 cup Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 2/3 cup chuño (potato starch)
- 2/3 cup sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla
- 2 tablespoons pisco
- manjarblanco (Peruvian dulce de leche) or jam
- powdered sugar
> BEAT: the whites until foamy, gradually add the sugar, then the yolks one at a time.
> SIFT: the flour with the baking powder and chuño and fold in gently, alternating with the vanilla and pisco.
> LINE: a 12-inch (30 cm) rectangular pan with parchment and grease it generously with margarine.
> POUR: in the batter.
> BAKE: at 350ºF (175ºC) for exactly 12 minutes.
> DUST: a kitchen towel with powdered sugar, turn the cake out onto it, quickly peel off the paper, spread with the filling and roll up.
=pudin-de-semola | Strawberry Semolina Pudding
- 2 3/4 oz (80 g) Nicolini semolina
- 1 can sweetened condensed milk
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) strawberries
- 4 eggs
- 2 cups (1/2 liter) water
> BOIL: the water and sprinkle in the semolina like rain, stirring with a wooden spoon.
> ADD: the sieved strawberries and the condensed milk.
> BOIL: 10 minutes, stirring constantly.
> REMOVE: from the heat and add the beaten eggs; pour into a buttered mold and bake in a water bath (baño maría) until golden.

=torta-helada | Layered Gelatin "Ice-Box" Cake
## SPONGE CAKE
- 2/3 cup Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 2/3 cup chuño (potato starch)
- 4 eggs
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 small glass pisco or lime juice
## ICE-BOX CAKE
- 1 can evaporated milk, chilled in advance
- 8 maraschino cherries
- 6 sheets colapez (gelatin)
- 1 box strawberry gelatin
- 1 box lime gelatin
- 1 can pineapple
- 1 lime
- 1 small glass sweet wine
> SPONGE — BEAT: the whites until stiff, add the sugar little by little and then the yolks one at a time.
> SIFT: the flour with the baking powder and fold gently into the eggs; pour into a greased 10-inch (25 cm) pan.
> BAKE: at 350ºF (175ºC) for 3/4 hour; remove and cool. The dessert is assembled in this same pan:
> SOAK: 3 sheets of colapez for 5 minutes, drain and dissolve with the strawberry gelatin in 1 cup boiling water and 2 cups cold; pour into the dampened pan.
> CHILL: until firm. Soak the other sheets of colapez, drain and mix with the lime gelatin, dissolved like the first.
> POUR: one-third of this cream over the strawberry gelatin in the pan:
> WHIP: the milk until foamy and add the lime and the cold lime gelatin prepared earlier. On top goes half the cake soaked in the sweet wine, more lime cream, the other half of the cake and the rest of the cream.
> REFRIGERATE: 8 hours; it's best to make the cake the day before.
> UNMOLD: by running a knife around the edge and dipping the pan in hot water just 1/2 inch (1 cm) deep.
> TURN OUT: onto a dampened platter and decorate with the pineapple and cherries.

=torta-de-semola-con-frutas | Semolina Cake Layered with Stewed Fruit
- 1 quart (1 liter) milk
- 8 tablespoons Nicolini semolina
- 2 eggs
- grated zest of 1 lime
- 1 tablespoon butter
- 5 tablespoons sugar
- 3 1/2 oz (100 g) raisins
- 3 1/2 oz (100 g) candied fruit
- 1 small glass rum
- 2 pears
- 2 apples
- cinnamon
- jam to taste
- whole cloves
> CLEAN: the fruit, cut it in small pieces and cook with the raisins, butter, candied fruit, rum, cinnamon and sugar for 10 minutes.
> BOIL: the milk with the sugar and lime zest, sprinkle in the semolina, stirring constantly to avoid lumps, and cook 10 minutes; remove and add the beaten yolks.
> LAYER: in a mold rinsed with cold water: a layer of semolina, one of fruit, and a final layer of semolina. Decorate with jam.

=turron-de-dona-pepa | Doña Pepa's Anise Nougat Bars with Spiced Syrup
## DOUGH
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) Nicolini "Harina Preparada" (self-rising flour)
- 1/2 lb (1/4 kilo) lard
- 1 tablespoon whole aniseed, steeped in 1/2 cup boiling water
- 1 tablespoon anise
- a few drops yellow food coloring
## SYRUP
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) light brown sugar
- 1 ball chancaca (raw cane sugar)
- 1 cinnamon stick
- orange peel
- cloves, anise, water
- 1 quince
> MIX: the flour with the anise and lard, add the cooled anise infusion and knead just enough to combine; add the coloring.
> SHAPE: into sticks the thickness of a thin finger and arrange on a baking sheet.
> BAKE: at 300ºF (150ºC) for 25 minutes.
> PUT: all the syrup ingredients in a saucepan with water to cover. Simmer (strain after 10 minutes), return to the heat and cook to the soft-ball stage (240ºF on a thermometer); let cool slightly.
> LAYER: the sticks with the syrup to build the turrón and decorate with colored sprinkles.

=torta-la-emperatriz | "Empress" Layer Cake with Peaches
- 4 1/2 oz (130 g) Nicolini "Harina Preparada" (self-rising flour)
- 2 1/2 oz (70 g) chuño (potato starch)
- 7 eggs
- 7 oz (200 g) powdered sugar
- 1 3/4 oz (50 g) melted butter
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
## BUTTERCREAM
- 8 oz (230 g) butter
- 1 lb (460 g) powdered sugar
- 1 cup cold milk
- 1 tablespoon vanilla
- 1 can peaches
> BEAT: the yolks with the sugar until thick and creamy.
> BEAT: the whites until stiff and fold into the yolks; then add the vanilla, the flour sifted with the chuño, and the cooled melted butter.
> DIVIDE: among 3 buttered, floured pans and bake 20 minutes or more.
> PUT: the milk and vanilla in a small saucepan and reduce to 1/2 cup; then cool.
> BEAT: the butter, adding the sugar little by little, alternating with the milk.
> FILL: between the 3 cake layers with buttercream and peach slices, then frost the top and sides of the cake.
