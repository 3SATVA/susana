=crema-de-esparragos | Cream of Asparagus Soup
- 1 small can asparagus
- 3 tablespoons margarine
- 4 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 1 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" beef bouillon cube
- 5 cups water
- salt and pepper
- 1 cup evaporated milk
- 3 tablespoons Parmesan cheese
> SEPARATE: the asparagus tips from the stalks and blend the stalks with their liquid.
> HEAT: the margarine in a pot and add the flour, then the asparagus with enough water to make 5 cups of liquid, and add the bouillon cube.
> SEASON: with salt and pepper. Boil one minute and add the Parmesan and asparagus tips.
> SERVE: at once.
* The evaporated milk is listed but not mentioned in the steps; stir it in at the end.
=crema-de-espinacas | Cream of Spinach Soup
- 4 bunches spinach
- 2 tablespoons margarine
- 2 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 3 cups water
- 1 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" chicken bouillon cube
- 2 cups milk
- salt and pepper to taste
- 3 tablespoons Parmesan cheese
- fried bread cubes
> WASH: the spinach leaves well and cook in very little water; when cooked, blend.
> MELT: the margarine in a saucepan; add the flour, milk, bread and bouillon cube. Boil, and when it thickens, season, add the spinach purée, bring to a boil and add the Parmesan.
> SERVE: at once with fried bread.
=crema-de-frijoles | Cream of Black Bean Soup
- 1 tablespoon margarine
- 10 1/2 oz (300 g) black beans, soaked overnight
- 2 tablespoons tomato purée
- 1 3/4 oz (50 g) bacon
- 1/2 cup undiluted evaporated milk
- 1/2 tomato
- 1 medium onion
- 4 quarts (4 liters) water
- 2 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" beef bouillon cubes
- fried bread
- salt and pepper
> BOIL: the beans in water with pepper, pieces of bacon and the bouillon cubes; when cooked, push through a strainer.
> FRY: the aderezo: finely chopped onion, margarine, finely cut tomato, salt and pepper and bacon in small pieces; mix all this with the strained beans, add the tomato purée and bring to a boil. When serving, add the milk and the fried bread pieces.
=crema-de-lentejas | Cream of Lentil Soup
- 1 cup lentils
- 1 tablespoon margarine
- 1 onion
- 1/2 cup chopped bacon
- 1 teaspoon crushed garlic
- 1 tablespoon tomato sauce
- 1 tablespoon Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 1 quart (1 liter) water
- 1 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" chicken bouillon cube
- salt and pepper
- small fried bread cubes
> BOIL: the lentils in water; when cooked, purée in the blender.
> BROWN: the chopped bacon in the margarine with the onion, garlic, salt and pepper; add the tomato sauce, the quart of water, bouillon cube and purée, then the flour dissolved in a little cold water; boil 1 minute.
> SERVE: with fried bread.
=crema-de-pallares | Cream of Lima Bean Soup
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) large lima beans (pallares), soaked overnight
- 1 tablespoon margarine
- 1/2 onion, very finely chopped
- 3 cups water
- 1 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" chicken bouillon cube
- 1 cup undiluted evaporated milk
- salt, pepper, nutmeg
- small fried bread cubes
> PEEL: the lima beans, then boil them and blend to a purée.
> HEAT: the margarine in a pot and fry the onion; add the purée, water, salt, pepper, nutmeg and bouillon cube. Bring to a boil, then add the milk. Serve with fried bread.
=crema-de-tomate | Cream of Tomato Soup
- 2 tablespoons margarine
- 3 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- salt and pepper
- 2 cups milk
- a pinch of baking soda
- 3 cups fresh, ripe tomatoes
- 1/2 onion, chopped
- 1 stalk celery
- 1/2 teaspoon sugar
- bread fried in margarine
> QUARTER: the tomatoes (three cups), chop the onion, and cook them with the celery and sugar for 10 minutes.
> STRAIN: this mixture and add the baking soda; melt the margarine and add the flour, salt and pepper.
> ADD: the milk little by little, stirring constantly until thick. Add this sauce to the prepared tomato liquid. Boil one minute.
> SERVE: with fried bread.
=crema-de-verduras | Cream of Vegetable Soup
- 8 cups water
- 4 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" beef bouillon cubes
- 1/2 cup chopped green beans
- 1/2 cup peas
- 1/2 cup chopped carrots
- 1/2 cup chopped celery
- 1 leek, sliced
- 1/2 cup chopped winter squash
- salt and pepper
- fried bread
> PUT: the water and bouillon cubes in a pot; when it boils, add the chopped vegetables and cook over low heat until tender.
> BLEND: to a cream and season (if needed, add a little flour dissolved in cold water to thicken). Serve with fried bread.
=crema-de-zapallo | Cream of Squash Soup
- 2 1/4 lb (1 kilo) zapallo macre (Peruvian winter squash)
- 3 tablespoons margarine
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 1/4 teaspoon crushed garlic
- salt and pepper
- 6 cups water
- 2 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" beef bouillon cubes
- 3 tablespoons grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 small can evaporated milk
- Nicolini "Harina Especial" (enough to thicken)
- fried bread
> PUT: the water and the squash in small pieces in a pot; when cooked, blend or sieve.
> FRY: the onion and garlic in margarine; add the squash broth and bouillon cubes and bring to a boil.
> ADD: if needed, the flour dissolved in cold water, and the Parmesan.
> SERVE WITH: fried bread.
> NOTE: if the squash is mealy (that is, good quality), the flour isn't needed.
* Zapallo macre is close to kabocha or butternut squash.
=chupe-de-camarones | Peruvian Shrimp Chowder
- 1/3 cup oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 1 teaspoon crushed garlic
- 2 tablespoons tomato sauce
- 8 cups water
- ground ají to taste
- salt, pepper, oregano
- 2 1/4 lb (1 kilo) freshwater shrimp (camarones)
- 8 yellow potatoes
- 1/4 cup rice
- 1/3 cup crumbled queso fresco
- 3 eggs
- 1/2 cup peas
- 1 cup evaporated milk
> WASH: the shrimp well and set aside the coral (roe/tomalley).
> HEAT: the oil and fry the onion, garlic, the coral diluted in water and strained, ají, tomato sauce and oregano.
> ADD: the water; when it boils, add the washed rice, peas and potatoes and let cook.
> ADD: the shrimp and boil three minutes, then the cheese. Just before serving, stir in the milk and eggs.
=chupe-de-pescado | Peruvian Fish Chowder
- 2 tablespoons oil
- 6 pieces white fish, floured and fried
- 1/2 can peas
- 1/2 cup corn kernels
- 4 tablespoons rice
- 2 tablespoons small dried shrimp (camaroncitos chinos)
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 clove garlic
- 3 eggs
- 8 cups water
- 1 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" beef bouillon cube
- 1 tomato, chopped
- 3 tablespoons crumbled queso fresco
- 1 large can evaporated milk
- oregano and salt
> HEAT: the oil and fry the onion, garlic and oregano; add the water and bouillon cube, and when it boils add the rice, peas, dried shrimp, potatoes and corn; cook over low heat.
> ADD: the cheese, the beaten eggs and the milk.
> SERVE: with a piece of fried fish and chopped cilantro in each bowl.
* Potatoes and cilantro appear in the steps but not in the ingredient list; the tomato is listed but not used in the steps.
=chupin-de-pescado | Fish Stew with Potatoes (Chupín)
- 3 1/3 lb (1 1/2 kilos) fish
- 1/2 cup oil
- 2 cups onion, sliced
- 1 cup tomatoes, peeled and chopped
- 1 heaping teaspoon crushed garlic
- 1 stalk celery
- 1 tablespoon chopped parsley
- 1 bay leaf, salt and pepper
- 3 tablespoons ground red ají (ají panca)
- 3 cups fish broth
- 8 yellow potatoes
- 1 rocoto pepper and a sprig of cilantro
- Nicolini "Harina Especial" for the fish
> MAKE: a broth with the head, tail and backbone of the fish, adding celery; strain and measure three cups.
> HEAT: the oil and fry the onion, garlic, tomato and ají; once cooked, add the bay leaf, oregano, salt and pepper, then the broth, potatoes and rocoto, and simmer over low heat.
> SEASON: the fish fillets, flour them and fry until nicely golden; add them to the chupín, bring to a boil, and serve sprinkled with parsley.
=menestron | Peruvian Minestrone with Basil Pesto
- 8 cups water
- 4 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" beef bouillon cubes
- 1 carrot
- 1 turnip
- 2 ears of corn, in rounds
- 1 cup chopped cabbage
- 1/3 cup peas
- 1/2 cup fava beans
- 1/3 cup fresh green beans (shelling beans)
- 1/4 cup green beans
- 3 white potatoes, diced
- 7 oz (200 g) Nicolini tube pasta ("canuto")
- 2 tablespoons oil
- 1/4 cup ground basil
- 1/3 cup crumbled queso fresco
- 1/2 teaspoon crushed garlic
- 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese
> PUT: the water, bouillon cubes and chopped vegetables in a saucepan; when half cooked, add the potatoes and pasta.
> MIX: the oil, garlic, cheese and basil in a bowl, add to the soup and boil one minute. Serve with Parmesan.
=sopa-de-arroz | Rice Soup with Ham
- 2 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" beef bouillon cubes
- 8 cups water
- 3 1/2 oz (100 g) finely chopped ham
- 4 tablespoons rice
- 4 tablespoons Parmesan cheese
- 2 medium white potatoes, peeled and chopped
- 1/2 teaspoon chopped parsley
> HEAT: the water with the bouillon cubes.
> ADD: the washed rice, potatoes and ham. Boil until cooked; just before serving, add the Parmesan and parsley.
=sopa-de-choros-y-fideos | Mussel and Pasta Soup
- 4 dozen mussels
- 4 white potatoes, diced
- 1/4 cup oil
- 1 onion, very finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tomato, peeled and chopped
- oregano and 1 sprig mint (hierbabuena)
- salt and pepper
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 7 cups water (the mussel cooking water)
- 2 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" beef bouillon cubes
- 1 tablespoon chopped cilantro
- 2 eggs
- 1 small can evaporated milk
- 1 cup Nicolini small thimble pasta ("dedal chico")
> CLEAN: the mussels well and put them in a saucepan with water to cover; boil until they open, pour off and strain the broth, and clean the mussels.
> HEAT: the oil and fry the onion, garlic, tomato, oregano, salt, pepper, paprika and mint.
> ADD: the mussel broth, bouillon cubes, potatoes and pasta and let boil. Beat the eggs and stir them in, along with the chopped mussels, milk and cilantro.
=sopa-de-fideos-con-frijoles | Pasta and Bean Soup with Bacon
- 1 cup Nicolini ring pasta ("anillo")
- 3 1/2 oz (100 g) bacon, very finely chopped
- 1/2 cup chopped onion
- 1 teaspoon crushed garlic
- salt and pepper
- 8 cups water
- 1 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" beef bouillon cube
- 2 white potatoes, peeled and cubed
- 1 cup cooked beans
- 1 sprig rosemary
> FRY: the bacon; when golden, remove it, and use the same fat to brown the onion, garlic and rosemary.
> ADD: the water, bouillon cube, potatoes and pasta and boil until tender. Add the beans and the fried bacon. Adjust the seasoning and serve at once.

=sopas-hervidas | Cassava Soup with Egg
- 1/4 cup oil
- 1/2 cup very finely chopped onion
- 1/4 teaspoon crushed garlic, oregano
- 1/4 cup peeled, chopped tomato
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) yuca (cassava)
- ground ají to taste, salt and pepper
- 8 cups water
- 4 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" beef bouillon cubes
- finely chopped parsley
- 2 eggs
- small fried bread cubes
> HEAT: the oil and fry the onion, garlic, tomato, ají, salt, pepper and oregano; add the peeled and chopped yuca, the water and the bouillon cubes.
> SIMMER: over low heat until everything is cooked; stir in the beaten eggs and chopped parsley. Serve with fried bread.

- 2 white potatoes, diced
- 4 tablespoons very finely chopped onion
- 1/2 teaspoon crushed garlic
- 1 teaspoon paprika, oregano
- 8 cups water, 3 tablespoons oil
- 4 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" beef bouillon cubes
- 1/2 cup evaporated milk

=sopa-de-quaker | Oatmeal Soup
- 6 cups water, 2 potatoes, diced
- 3 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" chicken bouillon cubes
- 1/2 cup Quaker (rolled oats)
- 1/2 cup fresh fava beans
- 2 tablespoons oil
- 2 tablespoons very finely chopped onion
- 1/2 teaspoon crushed garlic
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- salt, pepper, chopped parsley
> HEAT: the oil and fry the onion, garlic, paprika, salt and pepper; when the aderezo is cooked, add the water and bouillon cubes, and when it boils, sprinkle in the oats.
> COOK: for 10 minutes, then add the potatoes and fava beans. Serve at once, sprinkled with chopped parsley.
* In Peru "Quaker" is the everyday word for rolled oats.
=sopa-de-semola | Semolina Soup
- 6 cups water, salt if needed
- 3 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" chicken bouillon cubes
- 6 tablespoons Nicolini semolina
- 3 tablespoons Parmesan cheese
> PUT: the water and bouillon cubes in a pot; when it boils, add the semolina in a thin stream, stirring constantly.
> COOK: over low heat for 20 minutes. Serve sprinkled with Parmesan.
