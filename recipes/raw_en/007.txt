=fideos-a-la-crema | Creamy Baked Noodles with Chicken
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) Nicolini ribbon pasta ("cinta") No. 41, cooked
- 2 egg yolks
- 3 tablespoons margarine
- 3 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 3 cups milk
- 3 chicken breasts (poached)
- 1 teaspoon mustard
- salt and pepper
- grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 cup (1/4 liter) heavy cream
> MELT: the margarine and add the flour, then the hot milk (off the heat and a little at a time). Return to the heat, season with salt and pepper and boil 1 minute.
> REMOVE: and quickly whisk in the yolks (the heat of the sauce cooks them), the mustard and lastly the cream.
> TOSS: with the pasta and pour into a greased dish. Arrange the chicken breasts on top, sprinkle with cheese, dot with margarine and bake at 400ºF (200ºC) for 10 minutes. Serve at once.
=fideos-con-salsa-de-albahaca | Pasta with Basil-Spinach Pesto
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) Nicolini plain elbows ("codo llano"), cooked
- 1/2 cup margarine
- 1/4 cup oil
- 1 bunch basil
- 2 bunches spinach
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 5 oz (150 g) Parmesan cheese
- 3 1/2 oz (100 g) shelled walnuts, ground
- salt
> BLANCH: the basil leaves in boiling water for 3 minutes and drain.
> BOIL: the spinach, then blend all the ingredients; if the sauce is too thick, add a little of the pasta cooking water.
> HEAT: the sauce and toss with the pasta.
=fideos-con-salsa-de-aji | Egg Noodles in Creamy Ají Sauce
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) Nicolini egg noodles No. 41, cooked
- 1/2 cup margarine
- 1 large onion
- 1 teaspoon crushed garlic
- 6 fresh ají peppers, blended
- 1 large can evaporated milk
- salt and pepper
- Parmesan cheese
> MELT: the margarine and fry the onion, garlic, salt and pepper; add the blended ají.
> BOIL: the sauce 5 minutes, add the milk and Parmesan, toss well with the noodles and sprinkle with Parmesan.
> SERVE: at once.
=fideos-en-salsa-espumante | Pasta and Pork Ring with Foamy Cheese Sauce
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) Nicolini thick long noodles ("tallarín grueso"), cooked
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) ground pork
- 2 slices sandwich bread soaked in 1/2 cup milk
- salt, pepper, nutmeg
- 1/2 cup margarine
- 5 oz (150 g) grated Parmesan cheese
- 4 eggs
## FOAMY SAUCE
- 3 tablespoons margarine
- 3 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 2 cups milk
- 5 tablespoons Parmesan cheese
- 3 eggs
> MIX: the ground meat with the blended bread, cheese, salt, pepper and nutmeg. Add the pasta and melted margarine, mix well and fold in the beaten eggs.
> POUR: into a ring mold greased and dusted with breadcrumbs, and bake at 350ºF (175ºC) for half an hour.
> UNMOLD: and cover with the sauce:
> MELT: the margarine, add the flour and milk, season and boil 1 minute; remove from heat and add the yolks, and lastly the stiffly beaten whites and the grated Parmesan.
=tallarines-con-pollo-a-la-lombarda | Noodles with Lombardy-Style Chicken
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) Nicolini noodles ("tallarín") No. 41, cooked
- 1 plump, tender chicken, in pieces
- 2 cups grated Parmesan cheese
- 1/3 cup oil
- 1/3 cup margarine
- 2 cloves garlic
- 4 tomatoes, peeled and chopped
- 4 sprigs celery
- 1 tablespoon tomato sauce
- salt and pepper
- 1 cup peas
- 1 cup Sauternes white wine
- 2 carrots, grated
> BROWN: the chicken pieces in margarine.
> HEAT: the oil and fry the onion, garlic, tomato, tomato sauce, celery, carrot, salt and pepper; add 2 cups hot water, the wine, peas and the browned chicken, season well and simmer.
> TOSS: the pasta with half the Parmesan and the hot sauce, turn out onto a platter, arrange the chicken on top and sprinkle with the rest of the cheese.
* Onion appears in the steps but not in the ingredient list.
=fideos-a-la-napolitana | Pasta Napoletana

- 1/3 cup oil

- 1 large onion, chopped
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 1 teaspoon crushed garlic
- 1 medium carrot
- 1 stalk celery

- 12 botija olives, chopped
- Parmesan cheese

=fideos-con-camarones | Pasta with Shrimp in Tomato-Wine Sauce
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) Nicolini long pasta No. 39, cooked
- 2 1/4 lb (1 kilo) freshwater shrimp (camarones)
- 1/2 cup margarine
- 1 onion, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 large bell pepper, ground
- 1 cup peeled, chopped tomatoes
- 1/2 cup ketchup
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 1 tablespoon vinegar
- 1 bay leaf
- salt and pepper
- 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
- 1 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" chicken bouillon cube
- 1/4 cup chopped parsley
- Parmesan cheese
> CLEAN: the shrimp, remove the coral (roe/tomalley) and peel the tails.
> MELT: the margarine and fry the onion, garlic, bell pepper, tomato and ketchup; add the coral dissolved in water and strained, the wine, vinegar, bay leaf, salt, pepper, Worcestershire and bouillon cube; when the fat rises to the edge, add the shrimp tails and parsley and boil 3 minutes.
> REMOVE: toss with the pasta and serve sprinkled with Parmesan.

=fideos-con-pollo-a-la-andaluza | Melon-Seed Pasta with Andalusian Chicken Stew
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) Nicolini "pepa de melón" (melon-seed) pasta, cooked
- 3 tablespoons margarine
- 6 pieces of chicken
- salt and pepper
- Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 1/2 cup oil
- 1 cup chopped onion
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1/2 cup peeled, chopped tomatoes
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 3/4 cup cooked peas
- 2 red bell peppers, chopped (peeled and seeded)
- 1 bay leaf, 1 sprig fresh oregano
- 1 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" chicken bouillon cube
- 1 cup carrots, in thin strips
- salt, pepper and sugar

> TOSS: the pasta with margarine and serve the stew piping hot.

=fideos-en-anillo-con-salsa-de-pollo | Fried Angel-Hair Nests with Creamy Chicken Sauce
- 2 cups Nicolini angel-hair coils ("cabello rosca")
- 1 tablespoon salt
- oil for frying
- 2 tablespoons margarine
- 2 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 1 cup milk, 1 egg
- 1 cup chicken broth
- salt and pepper
- 1 cup fresh mushrooms, sliced
- 1 tablespoon margarine
- 2 tablespoons chopped cooked bell pepper
- 1 1/2 cups cooked chopped chicken
> PUT: the pasta in pieces into boiling water and drain. Grease 6 individual ring molds, press the pasta in and chill well.
> REMOVE: the pasta rings with the help of a knife and fry in plenty of oil until golden. Melt the margarine and add the flour, milk, chicken broth, salt and pepper.

=fideos-en-salsa-de-tomate | Long Pasta in Tomato-Bacon Sauce
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) Nicolini long pasta No. 34, cooked
- 4 tablespoons margarine
- 1 onion, very finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 3 1/2 oz (100 g) bacon, chopped
- 2/3 cup white wine
- 1 1/3 cups water
- 1 small can tomato paste
- 1 bouquet garni (bay leaf, celery, parsley)
- salt, pepper, a pinch of sugar
- 2 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
> MELT: 2 tablespoons margarine and fry the onion and garlic; add the bacon, wine, water, tomato paste, bouquet garni, salt, pepper and sugar.
> SIMMER: over low heat for 15 minutes; mix the flour with the remaining margarine and add it bit by bit to the sauce; boil one minute.
> SERVE: over the hot pasta.
