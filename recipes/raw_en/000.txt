=aji-de-atun | Tuna in Yellow Pepper Sauce
- 1/3 cup oil
- 1 green onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- cumin, oregano, salt and pepper
- 2 cans tuna
- 2 French rolls
- 1 cup evaporated milk
- 1/2 cup water
- 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese
- 2 fresh ají peppers, blended
- potatoes, olives, hard-boiled egg
> SOAK: the bread in the milk and water, then blend.
> HEAT: the oil in a saucepan and fry the onion, garlic, spices and ají.
> ADD: the blended bread once the aderezo (seasoning base) is cooked, letting it cook a few minutes more, then the tuna and the Parmesan.
> SERVE WITH: potatoes, olives, hard-boiled egg and fluffy white rice.
=aji-de-choros | Mussels in Ají Sauce
- 1/3 cup oil
- 1/2 cup chopped onion
- 1 teaspoon crushed garlic
- ground ají to taste
- salt and pepper
- 4 dozen mussels, cleaned and cooked
- 2 French rolls soaked in the mussel cooking water
- 1 cup evaporated milk
- 6 yellow potatoes
- 3 tablespoons grated Parmesan cheese
- 3 hard-boiled eggs, halved
- chopped parsley for sprinkling
> HEAT: the oil in a saucepan and fry the onion, garlic, ají, salt and pepper; when the aderezo is well cooked, add the bread, blended or pushed through a strainer.
> SIMMER: over low heat until thickened. Add the mussels, milk and Parmesan.
> TURN OUT: onto a platter, sprinkling with chopped parsley and slices of hard-boiled egg.
=aji-de-gallina | Shredded Hen in Creamy Ají Sauce
- 1/2 cup oil
- 1/2 lb (1/4 kilo) onion
- 1/2 tablespoon crushed garlic
- 6 green ají peppers, blended
- 4 slices sandwich bread (crusts removed)
- 1 large can evaporated milk
- 10 tablespoons Parmesan cheese
- 1/2 lb (1/4 kilo) walnuts
- salt and pepper
- potatoes, olives, hard-boiled egg
- 1 stewing hen, about 4 1/2 lb (2 kilos)
> SIMMER: the hen in salted water; when tender, drain, cool and shred.
> HEAT: the oil in a saucepan and fry the onion, garlic and ají; add the bread, soaked first in a little of the hen broth and blended.
> COOK: for 10 minutes, then add the ground walnuts, the grated cheese and the shredded hen; just before serving, add the evaporated milk.
> GARNISH: the platter with potatoes, eggs and olives.
=aji-de-huevos | Eggs and Pork in Ají
- 1/2 cup oil
- 1 2/3 lb (3/4 kilo) onion, very finely chopped
- 1 tablespoon paprika
- salt and pepper
- ground ají to taste
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) pork, in small pieces
- 1 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" beef bouillon cube
- 6 eggs
- 6 boiled potatoes
> HEAT: the oil in a skillet and brown the pork pieces; once cooked, add the onion, paprika, salt, pepper, ground ají and bouillon cube.
> BEAT: the eggs in a bowl and stir them into the well-cooked aderezo, letting them set quickly.
> SERVE: with peeled potatoes.
=aji-de-mondongo | Tripe in Ají Sauce

- 1/2 cup chopped onion
- 3 green ají peppers ground with two cloves garlic
- 5 ají mirasol peppers, ground
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) tripe, boiled and ground
- 1 sprig mint (hierbabuena)
- 3/4 cup evaporated milk
- 6 slices sandwich bread
- 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese
- salt and pepper
- 1/4 cup water
- 1 Nicolini "Doble Caldo" beef bouillon cube

> HEAT: the oil in a pot and brown the onion, ground ají, salt, pepper and garlic; when the aderezo is cooked, add the bread, bouillon cube, cheese and mint, stirring well until cooked, then add the tripe.
> SERVE: with potatoes, eggs and olives.
=anticuchos-de-pescado | Fish Skewers
- 1 medium toyo (small shark/dogfish)
- 1/2 cup vinegar
- 2 tablespoons ground red ají (ají panca)
- garlic, cumin, salt and pepper
- oil
> CLEAN: the fish and cut into 1 1/4-inch (3 cm) cubes.
> MIX: the remaining ingredients in a bowl, add the fish and marinate for one hour.
> THREAD: three pieces onto each cane skewer or wire and cook on a griddle or over coals.
> SERVE WITH: ground ají sauce.
=causa-rellena-con-atun | Layered Potato Terrine with Tuna
- 3 1/3 lb (1 1/2 kilos) yellow potatoes, boiled
- oil (enough to moisten)
- ground ají to taste
- lime juice or sour (Seville) orange juice
- salt and pepper
## FILLING
- 1 can tuna
- 1 cup mayonnaise
- 1/3 cup chopped onion (well rinsed)
- 1 hard-boiled egg, chopped
- 1 large avocado, sliced
- 6 olives, in small pieces
- chopped parsley
> PEEL: the freshly cooked potatoes and put them through a potato ricer; knead with the oil, ají, juice, salt and pepper.
> DIVIDE: into three portions. Use the first to line the bottom of an oiled 9-inch (23 cm) springform pan; top with half the mayonnaise mixed with the flaked tuna and the olives.
> NEXT: another layer of causa, then the rest of the mayonnaise with the onion, chopped egg, parsley and avocado slices, covering with the third layer of potato.
> REST: for one hour, then unmold and garnish as you like.
=cebiche-de-atun | Tuna Ceviche
- 2 cans tuna
- 1 large onion, thinly sliced lengthwise
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- pepper, and salt if needed
- lime juice or sour orange juice
- ground ají to taste
- chopped green ají pepper
- 1/2 tablespoon chopped cilantro
- boiled sweet potatoes, sliced
- corn on the cob, sliced into rounds
> FLAKE: the tuna into a bowl.
> ADD: the lime or orange juice, chopped and ground ají, garlic, onion, pepper and salt; let stand 1/2 hour.
> SERVE: sprinkled with cilantro, with the corn and sweet potatoes alongside.
=cebiche-de-pejerreyes | Silverside Ceviche
- 3 dozen pejerreyes (silverside fish)
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) onion, finely chopped
- 1 cup lime juice plus the juice of 1/2 orange
- 1 tablespoon ground ají amarillo
- 5 ají limo peppers, chopped
- 2 tablespoons chopped parsley
- 1 tablespoon crushed garlic
- 1 teaspoon pepper
- 1 tablespoon salt
- 1/2 cup chopped celery
## GARNISH
- 1 head butter lettuce
- 2 1/4 lb (1 kilo) yellow or purple sweet potatoes, boiled
- 6 large ears of corn, boiled
> WASH: the fish with lime and remove the bones; combine the onion, ají, parsley, garlic, salt, pepper, celery and lime juice.
> COMBINE: everything and add it to the fish; let marinate for about 1/2 hour, then roll the fish up around a filling of the onion and celery.
> SERVE: with sweet potato, corn and lettuce.
=cebiche-de-toyo | Toyo (Dogfish) Ceviche
- 2 1/4 lb (1 kilo) toyo fillet
- 1 large onion, thinly sliced lengthwise
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- salt and pepper
- 1/2 cup lime juice
- 1/2 cup sour orange juice
- 1 fresh ají pepper, chopped
- ground ají to taste
- chopped cilantro
- corn, boiled sweet potatoes, etc.
> WASH: the fish well, cut into cubes, season with salt and pepper and cover with the juices; leave for one hour.

> SPRINKLE: with chopped cilantro and serve with corn, etc.
=cebollas-rellenas | Stuffed Onions
- 6 white onions, peeled and boiled in salted water
## FILLING
- 1/2 lb (1/4 kilo) ground pork
- 1 teaspoon chopped onion
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 2 tablespoons oil
- 2 eggs
- 2 tablespoons Parmesan cheese
- 1/2 cup breadcrumbs, soaked in milk
- 1/4 cup milk
- salt and pepper
> TAKE: the prepared onions and scoop out the centers with a teaspoon.
> PUT: the oil in a small saucepan over the heat and fry the onion, garlic and meat; add the cheese.
> COMBINE: with the eggs off the heat, then stuff the onions.
> BAKE: for 15 minutes at 350ºF (175ºC).
=coliflor-al-graten | Cauliflower Gratin
- 1 young cauliflower
- 3 tablespoons margarine
- 6 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 3 cups milk
- salt and pepper, nutmeg
- 3 eggs, beaten
- 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese
- 1 tablespoon chopped parsley
> BOIL: the cauliflower in salted water with a piece of bread; when cooked, drain and cut into florets.
> MELT: the margarine in a saucepan, add the flour and, off the heat, the hot milk.
> SEASON: with salt, pepper and nutmeg, and return to the heat to boil for 1 minute.
> REMOVE: from the heat, let cool slightly and stir in the eggs.
> GREASE: a baking dish, arrange the florets sprinkled with parsley and pour the sauce over them.
> SPRINKLE: with Parmesan and brown in a 400ºF (200ºC) oven for 10 minutes.
=coliflor-en-salsa-blanca | Cauliflower in White Sauce with Stuffed Eggs
- 4 hard-boiled eggs
- 1 medium cauliflower
- 1/4 cup margarine
- 2 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 1 cup evaporated milk
- 1 cup of the cauliflower cooking water
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- salt and pepper
- margarine, evaporated milk
> CUT: the cauliflower into florets and boil in water with a little milk and a piece of bread.
> HALVE: the eggs, carefully remove the yolks, mash them with a fork, add the cheese, salt, pepper and a little evaporated milk to bind, and refill the whites with the mixture.
> MELT: the margarine in a saucepan and add the flour, then the milk mixed with the cauliflower water, salt and pepper; boil for 1 minute.
> ARRANGE: the florets in a greased baking dish with the stuffed eggs on top.
> COVER: with the sauce, dot with margarine and bake at 400ºF (200ºC) until golden.
=corbatitas-al-graten | Bow-Tie Pasta Gratin
- 1/2 lb (1/4 kilo) Nicolini pasta, medium bow-ties ("corbata mediana"), cooked
- 4 1/2 oz (125 g) margarine
- 2 tablespoons very finely chopped onion
- 3 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 1 1/2 cups milk
- 1 egg yolk, salt and pepper
- 1 tablespoon lime juice
- 1 tablespoon dry white wine
- 1 tablespoon chopped parsley
- 1 teaspoon vinegar
- 5 oz (150 g) mortadella
- 3 tablespoons ground crackers
- 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
> MELT: the margarine in a pot, fry the onion, then add the flour, milk, salt and pepper.
> COOK: until it boils for one minute, season, remove from heat and add the yolk, lime juice, wine, vinegar and parsley.
> MIX: with the pasta and mortadella; grease a rectangular dish and pour in the mixture.
> SPRINKLE: with cracker crumbs and Parmesan and bake at 350ºF (175ºC) for 30 minutes.
=croquetas-de-atun | Tuna Croquettes
- 3 tablespoons margarine
- 1/3 cup Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 2 level teaspoons salt
- 1 cup milk
- 2 cans tuna
- 1 tablespoon chopped onion
- 2 tablespoons chopped parsley
- 2 tablespoons lime juice
- 1/3 cup breadcrumbs
- 1 egg, oil
> MELT: the margarine, add the flour and milk, season with salt and pepper to make a thick white sauce, and let it cool; then add the remaining ingredients (up to and including the lime juice).

=choclos-a-la-huancaina | Corn on the Cob with Huancaína Cheese Sauce
- 6 tender ears of corn (choclo)
- 7 oz (200 g) queso fresco
- 3 fresh ají peppers
- a pinch of palillo (Peruvian turmeric)
- salt and pepper
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1/2 cup oil
- 3 hard-boiled eggs
- 6 botija olives
- lettuce leaves
> BLEND: the cheese with the milk; season with salt, pepper and palillo.
> HEAT: the oil in a pot, add the blended mixture and stir constantly until the sauce comes together (add a little more milk if needed).
> BOIL: the corn in water with sugar and a few drops of lime juice; when cooked, drain.
> ARRANGE: on a platter, cover with the sauce, garnish with egg slices and olives, and set lettuce leaves around the edge.
=empanadas-chilenas | Chilean-Style Beef Empanadas
## DOUGH
- 2 cups Nicolini flour (1 self-rising "Preparada" and 1 all-purpose "Especial")
- 4 oz margarine
- 1 pinch salt
- 1 egg yolk
- enough water to knead
## FILLING
- 1/2 lb (1/4 kilo) ground beef
- 2 large onions
- 1 3/4 oz (50 g) raisins
- 1 tablespoon Nicolini "Harina Especial" dissolved in half a cup of water
- 1 clove garlic
- 1/2 teaspoon whole cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon ground ají
- salt
- 1/2 teaspoon paprika
- 12 green olives
> PLACE: the flour in a ring on a kneading board, with the margarine, salt and egg yolk in the center.
> KNEAD: gently until the dough is neither too firm nor too soft; roll it out to a medium thickness and cut rectangles of about 5 inches (13 cm).
> PLACE: a portion of filling, 1 olive and a piece of hard-boiled egg on each; fold the dough over and crimp the edge with your fingers.
> FRY: the empanadas in plenty of hot oil and, if you like, sprinkle with powdered sugar and lime.
> (FILLING) CHOP: the onion into small dice and fry in a little oil, then add the meat, salt, cumin, ají and (washed) raisins, and fry everything together for 5 minutes.
> ADD: the flour and water, let it thicken, and remove from heat.

- 3/4 cup cooked peas
- 5 oz (150 g) English-style ham, chopped
- 1 cup well-seasoned mayonnaise
- 2 hard-boiled eggs

=ensalada-de-patitas | Pig's Feet Salad
- 3 pig's feet, boiled
- 3/4 cup oil
- 1/4 cup vinegar, juice of one lime
- salt and pepper
- 5 tablespoons ground fresh ají
- 1 large onion (soaked to mellow it)
- 5 radishes
- lettuce
- cooked yellow potatoes
> CUT: up the pig's feet and put them in a container.
> MIX: the oil with the vinegar and lime juice, salt, pepper and ají; cover the pig's feet with this marinade and leave three or four hours, turning them often.
> COVER: with the onion and sliced radishes and serve with lettuce leaves and potatoes.
