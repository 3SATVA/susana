=helado-de-invierno | "Winter Ice Cream" Custard Cups with Banana Meringue
- 1 quart (1 liter) milk
- 4 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 2 cups sugar
- 2 bananas
- 4 eggs
- 1 tablespoon butter
- 1 3/4 oz (50 g) cocoa
- grated zest of 1 lime
> MIX: the milk, flour, sugar (1 cup) and lime zest in a pot and cook over gentle heat for 10 minutes, stirring constantly. When ready, pour into individual glasses or dishes.
> MAKE: a stiff meringue with the whites and sugar; at the end fold in the finely chopped bananas.
> PLACE: a portion of meringue on top of each custard and decorate with cocoa cooked in a little water until thick.

=humitas-dulces | Sweet Fresh-Corn Humitas with Manjarblanco
- 6 ears of choclo (Peruvian corn)
- 1 cup milk
- 2 tablespoons margarine
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1 small box raisins
- manjarblanco (Peruvian dulce de leche)
> BLEND: the corn kernels with the milk; heat the margarine and add the mixture along with the sugar, cinnamon and raisins.
> COOK: over low heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture is glossy; remove and cool.
> TAKE: a corn husk, add a spoonful of the dough, a little manjarblanco, more dough, and close tightly.
> ARRANGE: on top of the corncobs with a little water in the pot and steam for 10 minutes.
! NOTE: dip the corn husks in boiled water to soften them.
=machacado-de-mani | Sweet Potato, Coconut and Peanut Confection
- 1 fresh coconut
- 1 medium sweet potato
- 1 ball chancaca (raw cane sugar; 2 cakes)
- 3 whole cloves
- 3 1/2 oz (100 g) peanuts, toasted and chopped
> GRATE: the sweet potato (peeled) and rinse it repeatedly to remove its starch; peel the coconut, save the water and grate it.
> CUT: the chancaca in pieces, add water to cover, the cloves and coconut water; when dissolved add the grated sweet potato and coconut. Cook to a thick consistency (you can see the bottom of the pot) and add the peanuts.
> REMOVE: let cool slightly, pour into a serving dish and sprinkle with ground cinnamon.
=manjarblanco-de-pallares | Sweet Lima-Bean Manjarblanco
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) pallares (large dried lima beans)
- 1 cup evaporated milk
- 3 cups sugar
- 1 large cinnamon stick
- 3 whole cloves
> SOAK: the beans the night before and peel them.
> BLEND: once cooked; pour into a saucepan and add the milk, cinnamon, cloves and sugar.
> COOK: stirring constantly until thick (you can see the bottom). Pour into a serving dish and sprinkle with ground cinnamon.
=manjarblanco-de-papa | Sweet Potato-Milk Manjarblanco with Walnuts
- 2 1/4 lb (1 kilo) yellow potatoes
- 2 large cans evaporated milk
- 1 2/3 lb (3/4 kilo) sugar
- 4 egg yolks
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1/2 lb (1/4 kilo) walnuts
- 1 small glass sweet wine
- ground cinnamon
> BOIL: the potatoes, peel and, while still hot, put through a ricer; add the milk and sugar and strain.
> COOK: until thick, stirring constantly (you can see the bottom of the pot); remove from the heat and add the yolks one at a time (keep stirring so it doesn't curdle).
> ADD: the vanilla, sweet wine and chopped walnuts; pour into a serving dish and sprinkle with ground cinnamon.
=mazamorra-de-chancaca | Spiced Chancaca Pudding
- 6 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 1 large cake chancaca (raw cane sugar)
- 2 cups water
- cinnamon, cloves, anise, orange peel
- 1 cup evaporated milk
- sugar to taste
- ground cinnamon
> CRUMBLE: the chancaca and put it in a pot with water to cover, the cinnamon, cloves, anise and orange peel; boil 10 minutes and strain.
> MIX: the flour with the milk, add the syrup and cook, stirring constantly, until done; pour into a serving dish and sprinkle with ground cinnamon.
! NOTE: how thick this pudding is depends on the amount of flour.
=mazamorra-morada | Purple Corn Pudding with Dried Fruit
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) maíz morado (purple corn)
- 1/2 pineapple with its peel
- 1 apple
- 1 quince
- 1 large cinnamon stick
- 3 whole cloves
- 1/4 teaspoon anise
- 3 quarts (3 liters) water
- 1/2 cup prunes
- 1/2 cup huesillos (dried peaches)
- 1/2 cup sour cherries
- sugar to taste
- sweet-potato flour (harina de camote)
- lime juice
> BOIL: the corn in the water with the pineapple peel, quince, apple, cinnamon, cloves and anise until very dark; strain.
> MAKE: a compote with all the fruit, water to cover and sugar to taste.
> DISSOLVE: the sweet-potato flour in a little water and add it to the strained corn water (with the fruit); stir until cooked (10 minutes), remove from the heat and add the lime juice; let cool slightly and sprinkle with ground cinnamon.
=niditos-de-amor | "Little Love Nests" Jam Sandwich Cookies
- 2 egg yolks
- 2 cups Nicolini "Harina Preparada" (self-rising flour)
- 1/2 cup powdered sugar
- 5 1/2 oz (160 g) margarine
- jam
> SOFTEN: the margarine, gradually add the sugar, then the yolks and finally the flour.
> ROLL OUT: the dough on a floured table and cut into rounds; cut a small hole in the center of half of them.
> BAKE: at 350ºF (175ºC) until lightly golden; remove, cool and sandwich with jam.

=pan-de-rosas | Rose Bread (Candied-Fruit Brioche Rolls)
- 1 lb (1/2 kilo) Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 4 1/2 oz (125 g) butter
- 5 eggs, 1/3 oz (10 g) salt
- 1/3 oz (10 g) fresh yeast
- 3 tablespoons sugar
- 2 tablespoons pisco
- 1/4 [unit missing] candied fruit
> KNEAD: put the flour in a ring on a board with the eggs, salt, sugar and the yeast dissolved in 1/2 cup warm water in the center; knead gently for about 1/2 hour, then add the butter.
> REFRIGERATE: the dough or leave in a cool place overnight; the next day cut off a quarter of the dough and line a medium mold with it; brush this lining with yolk mixed with milk.
> ROLL OUT: the remaining dough into a rectangle 1/4 inch (1/2 cm) thick.
> SPREAD: with a layer of candied fruit (soaked in pisco beforehand) and roll up gently.
> CUT: into 10 pieces, arrange in the mold and bake in a hot oven (250º) about 1 hour.

=pastel-de-fresas | Strawberry Tart with Gelatin
## PASTRY
- 2 1/2 cups Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 10 tablespoons powdered sugar
- 1/2 cup plus 2 tablespoons margarine
- 1 tablespoon orange-blossom water
- 1 egg yolk
## FILLING
- 1 package strawberry gelatin
- 1 cup boiling water
- 2 sheets colapez (gelatin), soaked in cold water and drained
- 1 cup strawberry juice
- 1/3 cup sugar
- 2 1/4 lb (1 kilo) strawberries
> PASTRY: sift the dry ingredients and combine with the rest; knead until the dough comes away from your hands and the work surface.
> LINE: one large or several small tart pans, prick the dough with a fork and bake at 350ºF (175ºC) until golden; remove, cool and fill with the following:
> WASH: the strawberries well and macerate 1 hour with the sugar; measure 1 cup of the juice.
> DISSOLVE: the gelatin in the boiling water, let cool slightly and add the colapez and strawberry juice; sweeten, pour into a shallow dish and let set.
> CUT: with a knife and cover the baked, cooled pastry with this gelatin; the strawberries go on top.
=pastel-de-manzanas | Double-Crust Apple Pie
## PASTRY
- 1 cup Nicolini "Harina Preparada" (self-rising flour)
- 1/3 cup lard
- 3 to 4 tablespoons ice water
## FILLING
- 6 apples, peeled and sliced
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 2 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 1 tablespoon margarine
- 1 egg yolk
> SIFT: the flour, cut in the lard with a pastry blender; when crumbly add the water, mixing with a wooden spoon.
> SHAPE: into a ball and divide; line a 10-inch (25 cm) Pyrex pie plate with two-thirds of the dough.
> MIX: the apples with the sugar, cinnamon, flour and bits of margarine and pour over the dough; roll out the remaining third, cover and crimp the edge.
> BRUSH: the top with yolk thinned with water.
> BAKE: at 350ºF (175ºC) for 45 minutes.

- 8 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 2 egg yolks
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- grated zest of 1 lime
- vanilla or cinnamon
- 3 tablespoons sugar
- 2 tablespoons butter
## PASTRY CREAM
- 1 cup milk
- 2 tablespoons Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 1 teaspoon grated lime zest
- 3 tablespoons sugar
- 1 egg yolk
- 3 Chilean apples
- butter
- 1 pinch ground cinnamon

> ROLL OUT: the dough to the size you need and fit it into a buttered tart pan.
> PRESS: the edges with a fork, making grooves all around and on the bottom. Bake in a gentle oven (250º) for 15 minutes.
> ADD: a layer of apple slices fried in butter and cover with the pastry cream; return to the oven to brown the cream.
> COOK: the milk, sugar, yolk and flour in a small saucepan, stirring constantly until thick.
> REMOVE: from the heat and add the lime zest.

=picarones | Sweet Potato and Squash Doughnuts with Chancaca Syrup
- 2 1/4 lb (1 kilo) Nicolini "Harina Especial" (all-purpose flour)
- 2 1/4 lb (1 kilo) sweet potatoes
- 2 1/4 lb (1 kilo) zapallo (Peruvian winter squash)
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 2 whole cloves
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon whole aniseed
- 1 quart (1 liter) water
- 5 oz (150 g) bread dough (as starter)
- oil
- chancaca (raw cane sugar) syrup
> BOIL: the sweet potatoes and peeled squash in the water with the cinnamon and cloves. Put the flour in a ring on the work surface and place the bread dough, sweet potato and squash with all their water in the center.
> BEAT: the dough well, add the anise and salt, and keep beating until it comes away from the table.
> PUT: the dough in a bowl, cover with a damp cloth and let rise; when it has doubled, heat the oil (in a deep skillet).
> TAKE: portions of dough with hands dipped in salted water and drop them into the oil, forming a large hole in the center; turn with a small stick.
> SERVE: with chancaca syrup.
