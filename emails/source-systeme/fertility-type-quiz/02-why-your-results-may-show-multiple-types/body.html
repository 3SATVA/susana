Why You Might Have Multiple Fertility Types

In my clinic and online practice, I discovered something very early on:

Women rarely show up with just one pattern.

Why?


Because fertility touches every system in the body:

  • hormones

  • digestion

  • blood flow

  • stress response

  • sleep

  • metabolism

  • detox pathways

  • immune function

When one area struggles, it ripples into another.

For example:

Liver Qi Stagnation Type
Stress (either environmental or mental) → poor gut & liver function → affects blood flow & methylation → affects womb lining → affects progesterone → affects early miscarriage risk.

Everything is interconnected, which is why women often score high in two or three categories. This isn’t a bad sign — it simply gives us a more complete picture of what needs support.
